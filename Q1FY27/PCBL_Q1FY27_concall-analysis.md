# PCBL Chemical (formerly Phillips Carbon Black) — Q1FY27 Result Analysis

**Quarter:** Q1FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) · **Reported:** 5 Aug 2026 · **Basis:** Consolidated · First report in archive.

## Verdict
- **Concall Signal: 🟡 Watch — Mixed Signals** (strong print, but management itself flags the margin is above-normal)
- **Management Credibility: B+** (candidly warned inventory gains will reverse — good candor)
- **Results Verdict:** Print **Strong** · Quality **Medium** · Trajectory **Rebound off a weak trough**

A big rebound — revenue +17% YoY, PAT +65%, OPM 16% (from 12%), EBITDA/ton ₹22,900 — but the quality flag comes from management's own mouth: **EBITDA/ton is well above the guided ₹16,500–17,000, and part of the margin came from inventory gains that "may reverse in later quarters."** Add ₹93cr/quarter interest (Aquapharm-acquisition leverage), and this is a strong-but-peak-ish print to bank, not extrapolate.

TTM NP ₹259cr / EPS ₹6.64. The QoQ looks explosive only because Dec'25 (PAT ₹2cr, 80% tax quarter) and Mar'26 (₹40cr) were unusually weak.

**Margin walk:** OPM 12%→16% on better pricing, richer specialty mix (specialty CB volume +23% to 19,748t), strong demand — **plus inventory gains management flagged as non-recurring.** Carbon-black volume 153,530t (domestic +15%).

**Quality check:** Medium. Other income negligible (good), tax 24% normal — but the *margin* itself is cyclically inflated (EBITDA/ton ₹22,900 vs ₹16,500–17,000 guide). High leverage: ₹403cr TTM interest.

## Guidance & Outlook
- **Carbon-black EBITDA/ton guided ₹16,500–17,000** full-year — i.e. Q1's ₹22,900 is ~35% above sustainable. Explicit "this normalises" tell.
- New **20,000 MTPA specialty line at Mundra** commissioned → capacity 900,000 MTPA.
- **Aquapharm** (water-treatment chemicals): current run-rate sustainable, slight dip next quarter on inventory, full-year similar.

## Key Investor Insights
- **De-rating factor:** Management-flagged margin normalisation (inventory gains + EBITDA/ton ~35% above guide) → H2 earnings likely step down from the Q1 annualised rate.
- **Growth driver:** Specialty carbon black (+23% volume) + Aquapharm diversification away from tyre-CB commodity cyclicality.
- **Risk:** High leverage (₹93cr/quarter interest) from the Aquapharm acquisition — earnings sensitive to both spreads and rates.

## Catalysts to watch
1. **EBITDA/ton normalisation** toward ₹16,500–17,000 — how fast the inventory gains reverse.
2. Aquapharm run-rate and integration.
3. Specialty CB mix shift + debt reduction trajectory.
