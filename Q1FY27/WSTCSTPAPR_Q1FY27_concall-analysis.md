# West Coast Paper Mills — Q1FY27 Result Analysis

**Quarter:** Q1FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) · **Reported:** ~11 Aug 2026 · **Basis:** Standalone table below; consolidated noted (includes Andhra Paper) · First report in archive.

## Verdict
- **Concall Signal: 🟡 Watch — Mixed Signals** (strong cyclical recovery, but commodity paper cycle + other-income flatter + Andhra Paper complexity)
- **Management Credibility: B**
- **Results Verdict:** Print **Strong** · Quality **Medium** · Trajectory **Cyclical recovery off a down-cycle trough**

Big recovery — standalone revenue +15%, PAT +166%, OPM back to 23% (from 14%) after the FY25 paper down-cycle (OPM had cratered to 9–10%). Consolidated PAT ₹141.8cr (+145% YoY) adds Andhra Paper. Genuine up-move — but paper is a commodity cycle (this same company printed 36% OPM at the 2023 peak and 9% at the 2025 trough), ₹24cr other income flatters PBT, and the Andhra Paper subsidiary (Kadiyam unit had a lockout, resumed July at 93%) adds moving parts. Enjoy the cyclical recovery; don't extrapolate it.

*Growth read: textbook commodity cycle in the ratios — 3Y PAT CAGR −36% and **ROCE collapsed 26→9** through the paper down-cycle. The +166% PAT is a price snapback; the returns say rent the cycle, don't own it.*

**Margin walk:** OPM 14%→23% on paper-price/realisation recovery + lower input (pulp/energy) costs. QoQ revenue dipped but margin jumped — pure spread/realisation.

**Quality check:** Medium. Other income ₹24cr = ~18% of PBT ₹137cr (treasury/investment income — recurring but volatile). Tax low at 20%. The margin itself is cyclically elevated vs the FY25 trough.

## Guidance & Outlook
- Paper realisation recovery is the driver; import competition remains the structural swing risk for the cycle.
- **Andhra Paper (subsidiary): Kadiyam unit resumed partial operations July'26 after a lockout, now ~93% of normal capacity.**
- Integrated paper/paperboard + duplex board; separate telecom-cable interest (WCCL).

## Key Investor Insights
- **Cyclical driver:** Paper-price recovery off the FY25 trough → margin snapback (14%→23%).
- **De-rating factor:** Commodity paper cycle with cheap-import overhang; other income flatters the print; Andhra Paper lockout/ramp adds risk. This is a cycle, not a compounder — 23% OPM is not a run-rate to annualise.
- **Standout:** Standalone PAT +166% — the down-cycle is clearly reversing; the question is how durable given imports.

## Catalysts to watch
1. Paper realisation/price trajectory + import pressure.
2. Andhra Paper Kadiyam ramp to full capacity + integration.
3. Whether 23% OPM holds or fades toward mid-cycle (~16–18%).
