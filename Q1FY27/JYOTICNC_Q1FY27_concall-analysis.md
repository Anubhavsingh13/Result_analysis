# Jyoti CNC Automation — Q1FY27 Result Analysis

**Quarter:** Q1FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) · **Reported:** 7 Aug 2026 · **Basis:** Consolidated (with standalone split) · First report in archive. Stock fell ~10% on the print.

## Verdict
- **Concall Signal: 🟡 Watch — Mixed Signals**
- **Management Credibility: B** (pending clarity on whether Huron drag is timing or structural)
- **Results Verdict:** Consolidated print **Soft** · Quality **Medium** · Trajectory **Decelerating (consol) / Accelerating (standalone)**

A tale of two businesses. Domestic (standalone) is firing — revenue +37% YoY, EBITDA margin 28.4% (+190bps), PAT ₹87.5cr (+21%). But the **French subsidiary Huron Graffenstaden** dragged consolidated PAT **−20% YoY** on revenue-recognition delays, and finance costs roughly doubled. The market punished the headline; the underlying domestic engine is intact. Which read wins depends entirely on whether Huron is a timing issue or a structural one.

**Standalone (domestic):** Rev +37% YoY, EBITDA margin 28.4% (+190bps), PAT ₹87.5cr (+21%). Consolidated PAT ₹57cr → **~₹30cr Huron drag**.

**Margin walk:** Consol OPM 24%→21% driven by Huron revenue-recognition delays (revenue booked without matching margin) + finance costs tripling to ~₹19–24cr. Standalone margin actually *expanded*.

**Quality check:** Medium. Other income clean (₹4cr), tax 22% normal. The softness is concentrated in one subsidiary's accounting/timing, not core demand — but that's also the ambiguity.

## Guidance & Outlook
- **Order book ₹4,848cr** (vs ₹4,732cr at Mar'26) — inflow ₹601cr, execution ₹485cr → **book-to-bill >1.2, healthy.**
- **New facility commissioning Sep 2026**, +10,000 machines/yr capacity — supports the domestic growth runway.
- Huron: management framed the drag as **revenue-recognition delays** (i.e., timing). Unverified whether it reverses.

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth driver:** Domestic demand strong — standalone +37% rev, order book still building, capacity coming online Sep.
- **De-rating factor:** Huron consolidation risk + rising finance costs. If Huron losses persist, consolidated earnings stay depressed regardless of domestic strength.
- **Standout:** The gap between +37% standalone revenue and −20% consolidated PAT is the whole story — the market read the headline, not the split.

## Catalysts to watch
1. **Huron reversal** — does the "revenue-recognition delay" unwind in Q2, or is it structural underperformance?
2. Finance-cost trajectory (tripled YoY) — one-off or new run-rate?
3. New facility ramp (Sep) and order-inflow momentum.
