# Sportking India — Q1FY27 Result Analysis

**Quarter:** Q1FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) · **Reported:** ~1 Aug 2026 (call 3 Aug) · **Basis:** Standalone · First report in archive.

## Verdict
- **Concall Signal: 🟡 Watch — Mixed Signals** (blockbuster print, but it's realisation/spread-led — volume was flat)
- **Management Credibility: B**
- **Results Verdict:** Print **Strong** · Quality **Medium** · Trajectory **Cyclical up-move**

Eye-catching headline — revenue +20%, PAT +123%, EBITDA +90%, OPM at a record 19% (+693bps). But the key tell: **yarn volume was essentially flat** (20,492 MT vs 20,327 MT). So the entire surge is **realisation + spinning-spread expansion**, i.e. cyclical. The structural offsets are real — solar cutting energy costs 12–15%, and the new Odisha plant adding 300–400bps by Mar'27 — but the base earnings power here is a commodity cotton-yarn spread, and this is a good point in that cycle.

- **Standout:** Record margin — but the quality is cyclical; the durable story is the capacity + energy build-out.
