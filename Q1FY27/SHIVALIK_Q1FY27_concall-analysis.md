## VERDICT (read this first)

**🟡 Watch — Mixed Signals (real consolidated turnaround, but it lives in a ~40%-held subsidiary; the core is weak; low returns + coming dilution + rich multiple)**
**Management Credibility Grade: C (Rent, don't own — low disclosure, low returns, opaque consolidation)**

Consolidated profit swung to **+₹14.97cr from a ₹2.84cr loss** — a genuine turnaround. But almost **all of it comes from Medicamen Biotech** (the pharma formulations subsidiary Shivalik holds only ~40–44% of), while the **standalone parent's PBT collapsed to ₹0.91cr (from ₹4.49cr)** — the agrochem core is soft. Layer on **structurally poor returns (ROE ~3%, ROCE ~5%)**, an **EGM-approved preferential equity + warrant issuance (dilution coming)**, and a **37.8x P/E**, and the risk/reward looks unattractive despite the optically strong quarter.

**The three things that matter:**
1. **The profit isn't really "Shivalik's."** Standalone PBT ₹0.91cr; consolidated PBT ₹16.58cr → the ₹15.7cr delta is Medicamen (formulations). You're buying a holding-company-like exposure to a 40%-owned entity, with minority interest to net off.
2. **The core (standalone agrochem) is weak.** PBT ₹0.91cr vs ₹4.49cr YoY — organophosphorus technicals under margin pressure (a theme across FY26: "profit surge masks margin pressure," "profit plunges 49%").
3. **Capital returns are poor and dilution is coming.** ROE ~3% / ROCE ~5% over 3 years; preferential + warrant issuance approved. Low-return business + equity dilution at a 38x multiple is a poor combination.
