# JNK India — Q1FY27 Result Analysis

**Quarter:** Q1FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) · **Reported:** ~Aug 2026 (call 12 Aug) · **Basis:** Consolidated · Chairperson Arvind Kamath, CEO Dipak Bharuka · First report in archive.

## Verdict
- **Concall Signal: 🔵 Thesis Intact** (strong YoY optics off a trough + seasonal Q1; order book is the real asset)
- **Management Credibility: B**
- **Results Verdict:** Print **In-line (seasonally weak)** · Quality **Low-Medium** · Trajectory **On-track to H2-loaded FY**

Headline looks explosive — revenue +81% YoY, PAT 8.5x — but read it in context: **Q1 is structurally JNK's weakest quarter (only ~10–15% of annual revenue)**, the PAT base was tiny (₹1.1cr), and **other income (₹6cr) is ~41% of PBT** in this low-margin quarter. Standalone EBITDA margin (14%) is in line with the full-year target, order book is ₹1,801cr, and management kept FY guidance unchanged. A fine, on-plan start — but the 8.5x is a base/seasonality artifact, not an inflection, and the stock slipped on the print.

## Guidance & Outlook
- **Order book ₹1,801cr** (~2x TTM revenue) — solid multi-year visibility.
- **FY27 guidance unchanged** — standalone EBITDA margin ~14% (Q1 standalone hit it).
- Q1 = only ~10–15% of annual revenue; H2-loaded on EPC execution timelines.

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth driver:** ₹1,801cr order book (fired heaters/heating systems for refining, petrochem, fertiliser, hydrogen) → visibility; 26% 3Y revenue CAGR.
- **De-rating risk:** Lumpy project execution; Q1 PAT flattered by other income; high working-capital intensity typical of EPC; premium recent-IPO (Apr'24) valuation.
- **Standout:** Reaffirmed FY guidance in a seasonally weak quarter — confidence signal, but the burden of proof is on the H2 execution ramp.

## Catalysts to watch
1. H2 execution ramp — does the order book convert on the H2-loaded profile?
2. Order inflows (book-to-bill) sustaining the ₹1,801cr backlog.
3. Operating-margin trajectory ex-other-income; working-capital discipline.
