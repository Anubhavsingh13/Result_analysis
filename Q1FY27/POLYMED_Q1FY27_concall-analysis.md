# Poly Medicure (Polymed) — Q1FY27 Result Analysis

**Quarter:** Q1FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) · **Reported:** ~Aug 2026 (call 10 Aug) · **Basis:** Consolidated · First report in archive.

## Verdict
- **Concall Signal: 🔵 Thesis Intact** (quality compounder; optical PAT dip is a high-other-income base effect, not operational)
- **Management Credibility: B+** (clear guidance + PolyMed 3.0 roadmap; minor governance note on CS resignation)
- **Results Verdict:** Print **In-line** · Quality **Medium-High** · Trajectory **Steady growth, margin normalising**

Headline looks soft — consol PAT −8.5% YoY — but that's misleading: the Jun'25 base carried an abnormal ₹43cr other income. On operations, revenue +30% and **operating profit +18%**, with strong international growth (+37%). Margins normalised (OPM 24% vs 26%) on new-capacity costs and mix. This is a steady medical-devices compounder executing to plan, not a stumble.

## Guidance & Outlook
- **FY27 guidance:** consol revenue ₹2,300–2,400cr, standalone ₹1,900–2,000cr; standalone EBITDA margin **25–27%**, consol **23–25%**.
- **PolyMed 3.0:** double revenue by FY2030 (~80% organic, 20% inorganic); high-tech/high-margin focus.
- Capex ₹200–225cr; Palwal plant online Mar'27, Noida (cardiology) Q1FY28. 6 new products launched in Q1; new CEOs (India/APAC, Brazil, Renal).

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth driver:** International +37%, renal/cardiology/critical-care expansion, PolyMed 3.0 doubling roadmap — a genuine multi-year compounding story.
- **Watch:** Margin normalisation (24% vs 26%) as capex-heavy new plants ramp; heavy reliance on other income in the PAT.
- **Standout:** Clear, specific FY27 guidance + a credible FY30 doubling plan — quality of disclosure is high.

## Catalysts to watch
1. Margin trajectory vs the 23–25% consol guide as new plants ramp.
2. Palwal (Mar'27) / Noida cardiology (Q1FY28) commissioning.
