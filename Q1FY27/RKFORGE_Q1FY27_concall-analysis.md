## ⚖️ THE VERDICT

**Concall Signal: 🔵 Thesis Intact (constructive lean)**
**Management Credibility: B — Reliable with caveats**
**Results Verdict: Print In-line/Strong · Quality Medium · Trajectory Improving**

A genuine margin recovery quarter — EBITDA margin at ~18%, the best in two years, up from the sub-14% FY25 trough — with near-term promises (debt reduction, exports, capex discipline) tracking to prior guidance. But the re-rating case is still entirely forward-looking: **ROCE is only 6%**, volume grew just **+4% YoY** (all the revenue growth is realisation/mix), PAT actually *fell* QoQ, and management declined to guide margins. The headline "PAT +297% YoY" is real but flattered by a depressed base. This call keeps the recovery thesis alive and on schedule — it does not yet prove the 20% ROC, ₹8,000cr-by-FY29 ambition.

**The three things that matter:**
1. **Margin recovery is real but the flow-through is leaky** — gross margin jumped ~535bps QoQ yet EBITDA margin rose only ~85bps (energy/opex ate the gain). Analyst Viral Shah caught this directly.
2. **Growth is price/mix, not volume** — revenue +20% YoY on forging volume +4%. The order book and exports are the engine, not utilisation (still 68% forging).
3. **Returns remain the whole debate** — ROCE 6% (was 19% in FY23). CFO guides 12–15% ROC this year → 20% by FY28. Everything bullish hinges on that ramp + rail-wheel JV + non-ferrous, all still ahead.

**The read:** FY25→early-FY26 was a clear trough (margins 13–14%, a loss quarter in Sep'25). The last two quarters mark a recovery — margins back to 17–18%, revenue holding above ₹1,200cr. **But two quality flags:**
- **QoQ PAT fell 16%** despite better EBITDA — because Q4FY26 carried a one-off **5% tax rate** (vs 28% now) and depreciation stepped up ₹11cr QoQ (new assets capitalising). Operating momentum is up; the reported bottom line understates it QoQ and *overstates* it YoY.
- **Margin flow-through is weak.** Gross margin rose ~535bps QoQ but only ~85–120bps reached EBITDA — energy and other opex absorbed the rest. Margin recovery is real but not yet efficient.

**Margin walk (QoQ):** + steel-price pass-through & richer export/mix (gross margin↑) → − higher energy costs & operating expenses → net +~100bps EBITDA margin.

**Results Verdict: Print In-line/Strong · Quality Medium · Trajectory Improving.** Still well below the 22% OPM / 19% ROCE of FY23-24.

## LENS 1(b) — Numbers Behind the Numbers

- **Volume vs price:** Forging volume **47,012 t (+4% YoY)**; revenue +20% → ~15pts is realisation (steel pass-through + mix + FX). *Underlying demand growth is modest; the story is pricing and mix, not tonnage.* Casting volume +85% YoY (new capacity ramp — small base).
- **Geography:** Domestic ₹735cr (+20% YoY), Exports ₹354cr (+11%). North America ₹222cr is the single largest export destination; Europe ₹128cr. Export mix guided to **35% of revenue in FY27 — highest ever.**
- **Narrative vs table:** Management leads with "PAT +297%" and "best period has just started." The table says: strong *margin* recovery, thin *volume* growth, and a QoQ PAT dip they don't dwell on. Emphasis is on the flattering YoY, not the softer QoQ/volume picture — normal, but worth noting.

## LENS 2 — Guidance & Outlook

**Hard guidance:**
- **FY29 revenue ₹8,000cr** (implies ~22–25% CAGR) — reaffirmed.
- **FY27 exports ~35%** of consolidated revenue; export growth "20%+".
- **Capex ₹350cr** FY27 (incl. ₹170.5cr press line, ₹20-30cr into rail JV).
- **Net debt −₹500cr** in FY27 → ~₹1,500cr by year-end (₹100cr done in Q1).
- **ROC 12–15% FY27 → 20% FY28** (CFO).
- **Rail-wheel JV (Titagarh):** 80,000 wheels confirmed for Indian Railways + 25,000 JV-partner obligation = 110,000 through FY28; JV launch Aug 2026.

**Soft/withheld:** Explicitly **declined margin guidance** — *"we would not like to guide the market in terms of profitability."* No hard domestic volume number ("robust… extremely exciting").
**Changes vs Q4FY26 call:** Debt-reduction target firmed (₹400-500cr → ₹500cr); rail-wheel scope *expanded* (40,000 → 110,000 wheels through FY28) — a genuine positive revision.

## LENS 3 — Management Language Forensics

**Candor: good on costs, guarded on margins.** Jalan is refreshingly plain that he won't promise margins — *"As an entrepreneur, we always see possibility of higher margins. Who does not want to earn higher margins?… We would not like to guide."* That's honesty, not evasion. He names energy as *"one of the biggest cost levers"* and ties it to geopolitics: *"Unless this geopolitical issue has some conclusion, I don't think we are going to see moderate energy prices."*

**Promotional tells:** *"The best period for RKFL has just started," "we are flooded… very exciting times."* Upbeat superlatives — consistent with this management's historically promotional register. Weigh against the fact that returns are still at a cyclical low.

**Internal tension:** Simultaneously "flooded with demand" and cautioning that energy/shipping/geopolitics remain unresolved margin headwinds — confidence on *order flow*, real uncertainty on *profitability*. That's a coherent (if hedged) position, not a contradiction.

## LENS 4 — Capital Allocation

The crux of the RK story. Borrowings climbed ₹1,207cr (FY24) → ₹2,449cr (FY26) funding a heavy capex + acquisition cycle (Mexico now in production), while **ROCE collapsed from 19% (FY23) to 6% (FY26)**. The pivot management is now selling: *"the majority of our strategic capex is behind us… focus shifted to higher utilisation"* (Khetan). Capex guided down to ₹350cr, net debt reducing, ROC targeted to normalise. **This is the deleveraging/returns-normalisation bet** — and Q1 delivered the first ₹100cr of it. Credible start; multi-year proof still owed. No dividend/buyback focus — cash goes to debt.

Cluster = **margins/returns**. Management answered growth questions expansively and margin questions with a wall. Not hostile, but the skeptics left without the number they wanted.

## LENS 9 — Key Investor Insights

- **Growth drivers:** Export ramp to 35% mix (record), rail-wheel JV (110k wheels through FY28, launching now), PV/2W diversification off a CV core, casting ramp (+85% vol).
- **Re-rating triggers:** ROC normalisation (6%→ guided 12-15%→20%) + deleveraging (₹500cr/yr). *If* delivered, this is the whole bull case.
- **Key risks / de-rating factors:** Energy-price spikes (unhedgeable), thin volume growth (+4%) exposing reliance on pricing, leaky margin flow-through, ring-rolling bottleneck, still-high leverage, ambitious ₹8,000cr FY29 target vs current run-rate.
- **Abnormal/standout:** Rail-wheel scope *upgraded* to 110,000 wheels; explicit refusal to guide margins (candid).
- **Catalysts to watch:** Aug'26 rail-wheel JV launch; H2 utilisation ramp toward 80-85%; whether QoQ *volume* (not just realisation) inflects; next-quarter debt trajectory.
- **Vs expectations:** Margin beat, PAT optically below Q4 (tax) — net a slightly-better-than-feared operational quarter.

### Bottom line
A **recovery that's on schedule but not yet proven** — RK is delivering on the near-term, controllable promises (debt down, exports up, capex disciplined, JV upgraded) while the returns story that justifies the valuation (ROCE 6%→20%, 22% margins, ₹8,000cr FY29) remains a forward bet resting on execution, energy prices, and a utilisation ramp that hasn't yet happened. **🔵 Thesis Intact, constructive lean; Management Credibility B.** The next two calls — utilisation ramp and rail-wheel commercialisation — decide whether this re-rates.
