**Margin walk:** −0.1% → 18% OPM YoY is entirely spread-driven. Orthoxylene feedstock spiked 50–60% in Apr–May causing demand moderation, but normalised by June to pre-Feb'26 levels; alkyd resin/paint demand strong.

## Guidance & Outlook
- PA spread: current **USD 250–300/t** vs 10-yr avg **USD 200–220**; management frames USD 200–250 as normalised range → *implies current spread is above-trend and likely to mean-revert.*
- **Anti-dumping duty extended 1 Aug 2026 for 5 years** — China ~USD 40/t, Korea ~USD 140/t. Structural margin floor. **Key positive.**
- Plasticizer plant (75kt) mechanically complete → commercial by **Sep 2026**, 15–20kt in FY27.
- CBG (waste-to-biogas) project start-up Oct–Dec 2026; FY27 contribution negligible, fuller impact FY28.

## Key Investor Insights
- **Re-rating trigger:** Downstream integration (plasticizer + CBG) shifts mix away from pure PA commodity — first volumes Sep 2026.
- **De-rating risk:** OPM 18% sits well above the ~11% norm on elevated spreads. Mean-reversion to 200–220 spread compresses earnings even with volumes flat.
- **Standout:** 5-yr ADD extension is the structural anchor under an otherwise cyclical name.

## Catalysts to watch
1. PA spread trajectory into Q2/Q3 — does it hold >250 or revert to ~220?
2. Plasticizer commercial ramp (Sep) — actual vs 15–20kt guide.
3. Whether 18% OPM sustains or was a spread-spike peak.
