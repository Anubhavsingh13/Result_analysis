# Newgen Software Technologies — Q1 FY27 Concall Analysis
**Call date:** 16 Jul 2026 (quarter ended 30 Jun 2026) · **Transcript/sources:** Company press release (PR Newswire, 17 Jul 2026); Q1FY27 earnings-call transcript (Investing.com); GuruFocus/Yahoo call highlights; Screener.in (consolidated) · **Speakers:** Diwakar Nigam (Chairman & MD), T.S. Varadarajan (Vice-Chairman & WTD), Tarun Nandwani (incoming CEO, eff. 1 Aug 2026, fielded most Q&A), Arun Gupta (CFO)
**Analyst note:** First report in the archive for Newgen — this establishes the baseline. Prior-quarter context reconstructed from public Q4FY26 (30 Apr 2026) disclosures.

## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🟡 Watch — Mixed Signals**
**Management Credibility: B (Reliable with caveats)**

A cleanly presented but genuinely two-sided quarter. Headline optics are good — revenue +11% YoY, PAT +26% YoY, EBITDA margin +170bps, recurring/annuity revenue now 71% of the mix — but the growth is off a soft base, the reported PAT is materially flattered by ₹36cr of volatile other income, implementation/license revenue actually *declined* on EMEA project delays, DSO stretched, and new-logo adds slipped to 10 (vs the usual 12–13). Management withheld formal FY27 revenue guidance for a second straight quarter, gave two inconsistent margin numbers, and dodged an RPO-disclosure request — all against the backdrop of a CEO transition taking effect 1 Aug. Nothing here breaks the thesis, and the recurring-revenue engine keeps compounding; but the explicit promise that "Q2 will cover the Q1 loss" makes the *next* call the referee. Hold estimates, don't raise them, and let Q2 resolve the implementation-recovery question.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **The print is better than the business ran.** Operating EBIT margin was ~13% (Q1 is structurally the weakest quarter); the 26% PAT growth leans on ₹36cr other income = **~44% of PBT** — treasury/forex, not operations. Underlying TTM revenue growth is only **~8%**, still a long way below the ~20% the company used to compound at.
2. **Recovery is a promise, not yet a fact.** Implementation revenue fell on "delays in project starts, particularly EMEA"; management says those projects "have now begun" and "Q2… will cover up for the Q1 loss." That is the single checkable commitment of the call.
3. **Leadership handover + no guidance = lower visibility.** Tarun Nandwani becomes CEO 1 Aug; the company still refuses a formal FY27 revenue number ("double-digit growth to continue") after guiding to only 6% delivered in FY26 vs its old ~20% aspiration.

## Key Investor Insights
**Growth drivers:** (1) Subscription/SaaS — subscription ₹146cr **+21% YoY**, SaaS **+40% YoY**; annuity ₹254cr (**71% of revenue**, +14%) — high conviction, this is the structural story. (2) US/Americas ₹92cr **+27% YoY** on subscription accrual — high conviction. (3) AI features embedded in vertical products (trade finance, insurance, lending, ECM) riding a central-bank AI-regulation tailwind — medium conviction, monetisation "still evolving." (4) India NBFC/trade pipeline recovery — low/medium, pipeline not bookings.
**Re-rating triggers:** Q2 implementation catch-up materialising; a formal FY27 guide being reinstated; RPO disclosure (would let the market price the subscription backlog); capital deployment (buyback/acquisition) of the idle cash pile.
**Key risks / de-rating factors:** (H) implementation/license lumpiness + EMEA macro-driven decision delays; (M) DSO stretch in EMEA — receivables/cash-conversion watch-item; (M) execution risk through the CEO transition; (M) new-logo slowdown if it reflects demand not just deal-size mix; (L) idle balance sheet earning treasury income instead of ROCE.
**Abnormal / standout guidance:** Two different margin numbers in one call — "23–25% EBITDA for the year" and "around 20%" — an internal inconsistency flagged by an analyst. Guidance *specificity* remains suppressed vs Newgen's historical practice.
**Catalysts to watch (next 1–2 qtrs):** Q2FY27 result → confirms/breaks the EMEA implementation recovery and the DSO-declining claim. FY-end (Q4) → the bookings/order-book number management only shares annually. Any RPO disclosure → confirms subscription momentum.
**Expectations read:** Stock fell ~3% on results despite the headline beat — the market saw through to implementation weakness, DSO and soft logos. Modest expectations; the debate is quality and durability of growth, not the level.

**Results Verdict — Print: In-line/Soft · Quality: Medium · Trajectory: Stabilising (after a sharp FY26 deceleration; recurring revenue accelerating, implementation decelerating).**

- **Margin walk:** EBITDA margin +170bps YoY (14.0%→15.7%) driven by "optimization of the AI practices in our engineering" and flat headcount (~4,200, slight decline) — genuine operating leverage, not one-off. QoQ margin collapse (34%→15.7%) is **pure Q1 seasonality** — every June quarter is the trough (license/implementation back-ended to Q4). EBIT margin ~13% vs ~31% in Q4 is the same seasonal effect, not deterioration.
- **Quality-of-results:** Medium. Tax normal (23%). But **other income of ₹36cr = ~44% of PBT** (treasury on the net-cash balance sheet + forex); note the same line was *negative* in Q3/Q4 FY26 (−15, −4), i.e. it is volatile and forex-sensitive — the 26% PAT growth is not all operating. Offsetting positive: mix quality improving (annuity 71%, subscription +21%, SaaS +40%).
- **Numbers behind the numbers:** Growth is **subscription-led, implementation-drag**. US +27%, APAC +12%, EMEA +10% (implementation weak), **India declined**. New logos 10 vs typical 12–13 — management reframed as deal-size up ("multimillion-dollar / ₹15–16cr+ deals"), a plausible but unverified mix explanation.
- **Narrative vs numbers:** Broadly honest. Management did *not* oversell — they named the implementation decline, EMEA delays, DSO and lower logos plainly rather than burying them. The one gap: the headline "PAT +26%" was led on without foregrounding the other-income contribution.

## 2. Guidance & Outlook
- **Hard guidance:** None on revenue — "expect double-digit growth to continue in coming quarters." Margin cited **inconsistently**: "23–25% EBITDA for the full year" *and* "we will surely maintain this 20% kind of number." R&D ~8–9% of revenue (continuity).
- **Soft/directional:** "Q2… we will be able to cover up for the Q1 loss"; delayed projects "have now begun"; "positive trend of DSO declining in coming quarters"; pipeline/bookings "growing at double digits."
- **vs prior call (Q4FY26):** Guidance posture **unchanged** — formal FY27 revenue guidance withheld for the second consecutive quarter ("cautiously optimistic," market uncertainty). This is a step down in accountability vs Newgen's historical ~20% USD-growth framing.

## 3. Management Language Read
- **Candor: good.** Problems named in the opening/early Q&A, not just when forced — "implementation revenue was hurt by slow project starts, especially in EMEA"; DSO openly acknowledged ("DSO is a challenge we recognize… because of the macro environment, certain payments were delayed"). Fisher's candor test: passed on the operational issues.
- **Hedging: elevated on the forward view.** "Cautiously optimistic," "coming quarters," "double-digit" — no numbers. The margin double-answer (20% vs 23–25%) reads as loose rather than evasive.
- **Deflection:** Confined to disclosure asks — RPO deferred indefinitely ("possibly in future… wait for some time more"); AI pricing "still evolving"; capital-allocation timing "deliberating." Not deflection on the *business*, but on *metrics that would increase accountability*.
- **Distancing / superlatives:** Low. Not a promotional team; if anything they under-claim. → Credibility **B**.

## 4. Capital Allocation
Net cash (reserves ₹1,636cr, borrowings ₹39cr; ROCE 25%, ROE ~20%). **Cash is building idle.** Acquisition "deliberating for the last couple of years… taking time to get the right fit"; buyback "given input to the board… they are working on that"; dividend "every year we try to improve." No commitment on any. Mild capital-allocation *drift-by-inaction* — a large treasury earning the very other income flattering the P&L, rather than being deployed into ROCE-accretive growth or returned. Watch-item, not a red flag.

## 5. Analyst Q&A Dynamics

- **Sentiment:** Neutral, professionally probing — not hostile, not deferential. The recurring worry is **quality/durability of growth and disclosure adequacy** for a business shifting to subscription, not solvency or governance.

## 7. Red Flags
- **Other income = ~44% of PBT, forex-volatile** — Medium. Not aggressive accounting, but reported PAT growth overstates operating momentum; use adjusted earnings.
- **DSO stretch (EMEA)** — Medium. Raised *and answered* candidly → concern lowered, but it is a cash-conversion item to verify next quarter.
- **RPO / AI-pricing disclosure declined** — Low/Medium. Refusing to disclose the metric that best captures subscription momentum, precisely as the model shifts to subscription, is a "raised-and-dodged."
- **Formal guidance withheld 2 quarters running** — Low/Medium. Reduces accountability; contrast with the old 20% framing.
- **New-logo slowdown (10 vs 12–13)** — Low. Plausibly deal-size mix; unverified.
- No governance, promoter-pledge, auditor, or related-party flags. Leadership change is planned/orderly (33-yr insider), not an abrupt exit.

## 9. Bottom Line
**🟡 Watch — Mixed Signals · Management Credibility B.** A structurally improving business (annuity 71%, subscription/SaaS compounding 20–40%, net-cash, 25% ROCE) had a *soft, seasonally-weak, other-income-flattered* quarter with real implementation and DSO drags, delivered by a management that is candid on problems but has stopped giving formal guidance and is mid-CEO-handover. Thesis is intact underneath, but too many items resolve only next quarter to call it strengthened. **Do not raise estimates.** The next call is the referee on three explicit promises: (1) Q2 implementation catch-up, (2) DSO declining, (3) double-digit growth sustaining — plus watch for RPO disclosure and any deployment of the idle cash.
