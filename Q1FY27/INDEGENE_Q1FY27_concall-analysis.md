## Verdict
- **Concall Signal: 🟡 Watch — Mixed Signals** (the profitability paradox)
- **Management Credibility: B** (transparent about the "investment phase"; must prove the margin-recovery guide)

EBITDA margin fell 20.2%→16.9%, PAT margin 15.3%→10.9%, on higher acquisition amortisation and an "investment phase." Strong top line, poor conversion.

*Growth read: the profitability paradox in the ratios — 40% revenue but flat PAT, OPM −400bps, and ROCE sliding 25→19. Growth is being bought with acquisition amortisation; returns are the casualty until the 19–20% margin guide delivers.*

**Margin walk:** OPM 20%→16% (−400bps) on elevated operational costs (investment phase) + **non-cash amortisation from acquisitions** (D&A doubled YoY to ₹44cr). Revenue growth is substantially inorganic.

**Quality check:** Medium. Other income ₹29cr = ~19% of PBT — strip it and PAT falls YoY. The flat headline PAT is actually *helped* by other income; core operating profitability weakened more than the print shows.

## Guidance & Outlook
- **Organic growth acceleration in FY27 vs FY26**, H2-weighted.
- **EBITDA margin recovery targeted to 19–20%** (from 16.9%) — the key promise to track.
- Near-term margin absorbs acquisition amortisation + integration costs.

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth driver:** 40% revenue growth + client-base expansion; strongest Q1 sequential in 4 years — demand is real.
- **De-rating factor:** Flat PAT on 40% revenue = broken operating leverage; margin erosion + acquisition amortisation. If the 19–20% margin recovery slips, the growth doesn't reach the bottom line.
- **Quality flag:** ₹29cr other income holds the PAT flat; core conversion is worse than the headline.

## Catalysts to watch
1. **Margin recovery to 19–20%** — the single number that validates or breaks the thesis.
2. Organic vs inorganic growth split (how much of +40% is M&A?).
3. H2 acceleration as guided.
