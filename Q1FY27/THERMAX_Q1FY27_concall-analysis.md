## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🟡 Watch — Mixed Signals** (strong order book vs a serious, *recurring* execution miss — leaning negative)
**Management Credibility: C — Rent, don't own** (candid and detailed, but the margin-discipline narrative was falsified within one quarter)

A genuinely ugly print: PAT −85% YoY to ₹22cr, consolidated operating margin **3.0%** (vs 10%), with **Industrial Infra swinging to a −₹71cr loss** on a ₹91cr project cost overrun, and standalone Thermax in an outright **loss** (−₹18cr). The overrun is framed as a one-project one-off, but it lands on top of a *multi-year pattern* — FGD legacy, NRL bioenergy, Green Solutions' contractor failure — and the deck itself admits most large Infra projects are running below as-sold margins. Against that, the forward story is intact and improving: order book **+23% to ₹14,045cr**, a maiden **>₹400cr US data-centre boiler order**, data centres now 17% of backlog, Chemicals recovering, net cash ~₹3,231cr. Cut FY27 estimates, but this is a *quality/execution* problem in the projects business, not a demand problem. One more overrun quarter tips this to Thesis Weakening.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **The margin-discipline story was falsified in one quarter.** In May the CFO called Industrial Infra margins *"fairly controllable… back-to-back tie-ups on all our bought-outs"* (Q4FY26 Q&A). Q1 delivered a **₹91cr cost overrun** and a −8.7% Infra margin. For a business that is *"almost all fixed-price"* (CFO), overruns are eaten, not passed through.
2. **Profit was carried entirely by treasury income.** Operating EBIT was ~₹9cr; after ₹35cr interest, core operations were **pre-tax negative** — the ₹68cr other income *is* the ₹42cr PBT. An abnormal **48% tax rate** then cut PAT to ₹22cr. Even normalising the ₹91cr, adjusted PAT ~₹95–100cr — still down ~35% YoY (lost ₹56cr PSI income last year, weak exports, Green Solutions overrun).
3. **Order book is the redeeming feature — but read the fine print.** +23% YoY, led by data centres (17% of backlog) and the US boiler win. Caveat: ₹139cr (≈5% of Green Solutions book) is a **TOESL order-book reporting-methodology change**, not new work; and the ₹1,600cr supercritical order (booked Q4) is exactly the long-cycle, lumpy EPC risk that has burned Thermax before.

## Key Investor Insights
**Growth drivers** — (1) **Data centres** — maiden >₹400cr US boiler order + cooling pipeline; 17% of order book — *med-high conviction, genuinely new vector*. (2) **Large boilers/supercritical** (TBWES) — MB Power ₹1,600cr + Dangote — *high revenue visibility, but execution/margin risk*. (3) Chemicals turn — PBIT 11.2% vs 9.3% on volume/mix — *low-magnitude but real*.
**Re-rating triggers** — a clean quarter proving the ₹91cr was contained; Green Solutions reaching breakeven; working-capital normalisation; data-centre order sizing/market-share disclosure (management has repeatedly declined to quantify).
**Key risks / de-rating** — **recurring EPC execution/estimation misses** (High) — deck shows majority of >₹100cr Infra projects below as-sold margin; Green Solutions chronic losses (Med); fixed-price + hardening steel/non-ferrous/tube prices squeezing Products/Chemicals (Med); Middle-East capex softening on the war (Med); profit dependence on treasury income (Med).
**Abnormal / standout guidance** — management **won't give hard numbers** (declined revenue guide, segment margins, DC market size, TBWES financials, ME exposure) — the one concrete reassurance (Infra margins "controllable") was the one that broke. Order-book *methodology change* mid-year is a scoreboard-shift to watch.
**Catalysts to watch (next 1–2 qtrs)** — Q1FY27 **transcript** (get the Q&A on the overrun) · Q2 Infra margin (contained or spreading?) · Green Solutions loss trajectory · working-capital reversal · data-centre order flow.
**Expectations read** — Stock −16% on results has already repriced the miss; the debate is whether execution quality in the projects business structurally caps margins. Data-centre/energy-transition optionality is real and under-quantified — a free call if execution holds.

- **Margin walk:** Infra PBIT +83 → **−71** (₹91cr overrun + loss of ₹56cr PSI income YoY); Industrial Products 8.3% → 6.1% (lower exports, higher steel/non-ferrous input costs, fixed-price); Green Solutions loss −5 → **−17** (further project overruns). ## 2. Guidance & Outlook
Management gives **little hard guidance** (a standing pattern). From Q4FY26: FY27 capex ~₹250cr (₹100–150cr maintenance + boiler/cooling line expansion); no revenue number ("order balance +27%… good opportunity on revenue," but "cautious" on war impact in Q2/Q3); order pipeline "reasonably robust." Q1FY27 deck: industry outlook flags **hardening steel/tube/non-ferrous prices**, strong PMI (>54), **accelerating data-centre demand**, and **softening Middle-East capex** on the war. No margin-recovery timeline offered for Infra/Green Solutions — a gap given this quarter.

## 3. Management Language Read *(Q4 FY26 transcript)*
- **Candor — reasonable.** Admits problems when pressed: working capital *"well observed… you're right"*; Green Solutions *"contractor failed to perform… same partner"* (Aditya Mongia flagged it as a **recurring** partner issue — CFO conceded). No stonewalling of hard topics.
- **But heavy hedging + serial deflection on specifics.** Near-constant "reasonably / fairly / broadly / I think"; and repeated *"I'll get back to you / can't quantify / give us some time"* on data-centre size, boiler pipeline, TBWES financials, ME exposure, DC capacity. Genuinely quantitative questions rarely get numbers.
- **The overconfidence tell:** the single firmest reassurance — Infra margins *"fairly controllable… back-to-back"* — is exactly what broke. → caps credibility at **C**, though candor/transparency is a redeeming B-grade trait.

## 4. Capital Allocation
Balance sheet strong: **net cash ~₹3,231cr**, comfortable. FY27 capex modest ~₹250cr (capacity debottlenecking in boilers + Sri City cooling for data-centre demand — sensible, demand-linked). Dividend ₹20/sh declared. **Amalgamation approved:** merging two wholly-owned subs (Thermax Bioenergy Solutions + Thermax Cooling Solutions) into the parent — group-structure simplification of a 51-subsidiary group; housekeeping, not value-changing. No capital-allocation drift; the concern is *returns on the projects business*, not where cash goes.

## 9. Bottom Line
**🟡 Watch — Mixed Signals · Credibility C.** Strip the noise and Thermax is two businesses: a solid Products/Chemicals/cooling franchise riding a real data-centre and energy-transition cycle (order book +23%), and a **projects business that keeps mis-estimating and overrunning** — the ₹91cr is the latest in a pattern the deck's own margin-variance table confirms. The demand thesis is intact and improving; the *earnings quality and execution* thesis is the problem, and it caps the multiple until proven contained. Cut FY27 numbers, size the position for execution risk, and on the next call resolve: **is the ₹91cr contained, does Green Solutions stop bleeding, and does Infra margin normalise?** A second overrun quarter moves this to **Thesis Weakening**.
