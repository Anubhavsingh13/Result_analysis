# Valiant Organics — Q1FY27 Result Analysis

**Quarter:** Q1FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) · **Reported:** 14 Aug 2026 · **Basis:** Consolidated · First report in archive.

## Verdict
- **Concall Signal: 🟢 Thesis Strengthened** (recovery confirming; verify sustainability)
- **Management Credibility: B**
- **Results Verdict:** Print **Strong** · Quality **Medium-High** · Trajectory **Accelerating**

Second consecutive improving quarter and the best OPM (18%) in the visible history, after a brutal FY24–FY25. The Pharma segment turning profitable (+₹8.99cr vs a prior loss) is the structural signal — not just a price/cost swing. Caveat: other income (₹11cr) flatters PBT, and the base was very depressed.

**Quality check:** Medium-High. Operating turnaround genuine (OP ₹42 vs ₹25 YoY). But other income ₹11cr = ~30% of PBT ₹37cr — strip it and the print is good, not spectacular. Tax 20% normal.

## Guidance & Outlook
- **Pharma division turned profitable** (segment result +₹8.99cr) — was a persistent drag; the key inflection.
- Better raw-material costs + pricing power cited as margin drivers.
- Governance/housekeeping: MD Sathiababu K. Kallada reappointed; registered office shifting to Tarapur. Routine, signals continuity.

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth driver:** Pharma segment inflection from loss to profit — if it holds, structurally lifts blended margin.
- **De-rating risk:** Only two clean quarters after multi-year weakness; 18% OPM is a step-change from the 12% run-rate — sustainability unproven.
- **Quality flag:** ₹11cr other income does real work in the PBT — watch operating-only trajectory next quarter.

## Catalysts to watch
1. Does Pharma stay profitable in Q2, or was Q1 a one-off?
2. OPM — hold at ~18% or revert toward 12%?
