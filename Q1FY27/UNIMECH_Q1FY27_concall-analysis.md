# Unimech Aerospace & Manufacturing — Q1FY27 Result Analysis

**Quarter:** Q1FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) · **Reported:** ~1 Aug 2026 (call 4 Aug) · **Basis:** Consolidated · First report in archive.

## Verdict
- **Concall Signal: 🟡 Watch — Mixed Signals** (record print + order book, but growth is acquisition-aided, business is lumpy, and a ₹750cr QIP dilutes)
- **Management Credibility: B**
- **Results Verdict:** Print **Strong** · Quality **Medium** · Trajectory **Recovering off a Dec'25 air-pocket, but lumpy**

Record revenue ₹108cr (+71% YoY), margin back to 36%, order book up 30% QoQ to ₹280cr — a strong rebound from the Dec'25 air-pocket (revenue had collapsed to ₹34cr). But read the quality: **~21% of revenue is the newly-acquired Hobel Bellows** (April'26, only 2 months in), so organic growth is well below +71%; the business is **highly lumpy** (Dec'25 OPM 4.6%); other income (₹7cr) is a meaningful chunk of PBT; and a **₹750cr QIP** is coming (dilution). Great franchise metrics, but the signals genuinely cut both ways this quarter.

## Guidance & Outlook
- **Order book ₹280cr** (up from ₹215cr at Mar'26); **nuclear order wins ~₹88.7cr cumulatively**, execution H2FY27.
- **₹750cr QIP approved** + ₹5cr into associate Dheya Engineering — meaningful capital raise/dilution.
- Aerospace tooling = 76% of revenue; Hobel Bellows acquisition (Apr'26) = 21% of Q1 revenue.

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth driver:** Order book +30% QoQ, nuclear/defence wins, aero-tooling franchise, M&A (Hobel) building scale.
- **De-rating risk:** Extreme quarterly lumpiness (Dec'25 air-pocket); +71% flattered by acquisition; ₹750cr QIP dilution; premium recent-IPO valuation; other-income reliance.
- **Standout:** Record revenue + order book rebuild after a weak Dec'25 — but underwrite the *trend*, not the single quarter.

## Catalysts to watch
1. Organic (ex-Hobel) growth rate — the real underlying momentum.
2. QIP terms/dilution and deployment.
3. Order-book execution (nuclear ₹88.7cr in H2) and quarter-to-quarter smoothing.
