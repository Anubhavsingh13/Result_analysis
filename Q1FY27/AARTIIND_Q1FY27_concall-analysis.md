# Aarti Industries — Q1FY27 Result Analysis

**Quarter:** Q1FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) · **Reported:** 31 Jul 2026 · **Basis:** Consolidated · First report in archive.

> **Correction (5-Nov-2026):** the ₹1,800–2,200 Cr EBITDA target below is the **FY28** target, the end of the FY25–28 three-year plan, not FY27. See the Q1FY27 transcript Q&A and the Q2FY27 report.

## Verdict
- **Concall Signal: 🟢 Thesis Strengthened** (margin/mix-led — volume recovery still pending)
- **Management Credibility: B**
- **Results Verdict:** Print **Strong** · Quality **Medium-High** · Trajectory **Accelerating (off a weak FY25 base)**

Sharp recovery — PAT +260% YoY, OPM at a ~2-year high (16%), EBITDA guidance ₹1,800–2,200cr reaffirmed. The important nuance: the beat is **product-mix, inventory and forex-led, not volume-led** — management explicitly flagged "volume pressures in a challenging global environment." So it's a genuine earnings recovery, but on demand that hasn't fully turned yet.

**Margin walk:** OPM 13%→16% on optimised product mix, inventory management and **forex gains** — not volume. Energy business temporarily hit by West Asia disruption, managed via diversification.

**Quality check:** Medium-High. Other income low (₹5cr) — profit is operational. But tax rate just 14% (vs ~25% norm) flatters PAT this quarter, and forex gains are inside EBITDA. Strip both and the underlying is good, not the full +260%.

## Guidance & Outlook
- **FY27 EBITDA reaffirmed ₹1,800–2,200cr** (Q1 run-rate ~₹1,530cr annualised → needs H2 ramp).
- Fuel-additives capacity **290→360 KTPA completed Jul 2026.**
- **PEDA & MPP** in customer qualification; MPP plants operational Q2FY27, scale over next 2 quarters.

## Key Investor Insights
- **Re-rating trigger:** New capacity (fuel additives, PEDA/MPP) commercialising through FY27 — the volume leg the recovery still lacks.
- **De-rating risk:** Recovery is mix/forex-aided on soft volumes + a low 14% tax quarter; extrapolating +260% PAT growth is a mistake.
- **Standout:** OPM back to 16% (2-yr high) while volumes are still pressured → operating leverage upside if demand turns.

## Catalysts to watch
1. Does **volume** finally recover (the whole recovery so far is mix/price/forex)?
2. H2 ramp to hit the ₹1,800–2,200cr EBITDA guide.
3. PEDA/MPP qualification → revenue conversion.
