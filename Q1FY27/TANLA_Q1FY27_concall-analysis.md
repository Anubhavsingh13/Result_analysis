# Tanla Platforms — Q1FY27 Result Analysis

**Quarter:** Q1FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) · **Reported:** 23 Jul 2026 · **Basis:** Consolidated · CEO Uday Reddy · First report in archive.

## Verdict
- **Concall Signal: 🔵 Thesis Intact** (growth returning after stagnation, with improving quality — but structural OTT/A2P overhang persists)
- **Management Credibility: B+**
- **Results Verdict:** Print **In-line-to-good** · Quality **High** · Trajectory **Re-accelerating**

After ~two years of revenue stuck around ₹1,000cr/quarter, Tanla is growing again — revenue +18% YoY, and importantly **gross profit +25% and EBITDA +23% grew faster than revenue**, signalling mix improvement, not just volume. PAT +20%, FCF ₹126cr (89% of PAT — excellent cash conversion). Margins are stable at ~16% (structurally below the 19–20% heyday). Steady, cash-rich, quality-improving — but the CPaaS/A2P-SMS commoditisation and OTT (WhatsApp) threat remain the long-term overhang.

**Margin walk:** OPM flat at 16% (well below the 19–20% of FY24), but GP/EBITDA growing faster than revenue = mix quality improving at the margin. FCF 89% of PAT is a standout.

**Quality check:** High. Other income ₹14cr modest, tax 22%, near-zero debt, strong cash conversion. Clean.

## Guidance & Outlook
- Revenue +18% "with gross profit and EBITDA growing even faster — improving quality of growth" (CEO).
- **Wisely.ai / ATP** (anti-spam/scam) platform traction — Indosat deployment (100M+ users), London Business School case study.
- Focus on "profitable growth that consistently converts into cash."

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth driver:** CPaaS re-acceleration + Wisely.ai/ATP platform (higher-value, stickier than plain A2P SMS) — the mix-up story.
- **De-rating factor:** Structural — A2P SMS commoditisation, OTT/WhatsApp substitution, margin already reset from 20%→16%.
- **Standout:** GP/EBITDA outgrowing revenue + 89% FCF/PAT — quality of the growth is better than the flat headline margin suggests.

## Catalysts to watch
1. Wisely.ai/ATP monetisation scaling (the margin-mix lever).
2. Whether GP keeps outgrowing revenue (mix shift sticking).
3. OTT/RCS substitution impact on the A2P SMS core.
