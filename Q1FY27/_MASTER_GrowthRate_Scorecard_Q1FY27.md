## What the scorecard reveals (that the single-quarter prints hide)

**1. The TTM-PAT-growth headlines are mostly base effect, not compounding.** Valiant +895%, Tatva +632%, JNK +202%, NGL +181%, Aarti Ind +122% TTM profit growth — all off collapsed FY26 troughs. The **3Y PAT CAGR** exposes it: *negative* for IGPL (−78%), West Coast (−36%), Valiant (−30%), PCBL (−21%), Balaji (−20%), Laxmi (−14%), Paushak (−13%), Aarti Ind (−9%), Sportking (−8%), Tatva (−5%), Rolex (−3%). Eleven of 23 have shrunk profits over three years despite this quarter's fireworks.

**2. ROCE is *declining* for a striking number — even the good businesses.** Sharp deteriorations: PCBL 14→8, West Coast 26→9, Poly Medicure 24→14, Paushak 17→8, Unimech 22→11, Balaji 17→11. Even quality names softened: Tanla 38→26, Indegene 25→19, Jyoti 24→21. Capex, dilution and cyclical margin resets are eating returns.

**3. Only five names pair high, stable/rising ROCE with real 3Y growth — the "own-through-cycle" quadrant:**
- **Stylam** (ROCE 27%, 3Y PAT +15%), **Sai Life** (20%, +226%, rising), **Jyoti CNC** (21%, +157%), **Aeroflex** (19%, +19%), **Tanla** (26%, +4%, high but easing).
- **NGL Fine-Chem** (17%, recovered 8→17) and **JNK** (17%) are the next tier up.

**4. The bottom third is a cyclical-recovery basket — rent, don't own.** IGPL, Valiant, Laxmi, Tatva, Aarti Ind, PCBL, West Coast: ROCE 3–9%, negative 3Y CAGR, huge TTM bounce. The Q1 margin spike is a spread/price windfall on a low-return base — the ROCE says the capital hasn't earned through the cycle. Enjoy the up-move; don't capitalise the peak.

## Bottom line
The single most useful cut: **cross the Q1 print against ROCE.** A great print on rising ROCE (Sai Life, IOLCP, NGL, Sportking, Tatva-recovering) is real operating improvement; a great print on *falling* ROCE (PCBL, West Coast, Poly Medicure, Unimech, Paushak) is growth being bought with capital that isn't earning — the print flatters, the returns don't. That distinction, not the PAT-growth headline, is what this quarter's scorecard is *for*.
