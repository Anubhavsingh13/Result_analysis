# Indegene — Q2FY27 Result Analysis
**Quarter:** Q2FY27 (Jul–Sep 2026) · **Results:** 29 Oct 2026 (call 30 Oct) · **Basis:** Consolidated
**Sources:** BSE press release and investor presentation (29 Oct); Q1FY27 transcript (Aug 2026); prior archive report `Q1FY27/INDEGENE_Q1FY27_concall-analysis.md`; Screener.in. CMP ₹620 (2 Nov, +5.5% on the day; 52-wk high ₹638), M-cap ₹14,942 cr, TTM P/E 35×.
**⚠️ The Q2 call transcript is not yet filed** (audio only). The language and Q&A lenses are limited.

## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🟡 Watch — Mixed Signals (improving; Q3 is the test)**
**Management Credibility: B** (the "stable through the wage hike" promise was kept; the 19–20% H2 margin promise is due next quarter)
**Results Verdict:** Print **Good revenue / soft profit** · Quality **Medium** · Trajectory **Revenue accelerating, margin stable**

Revenue **₹1,122 cr (+39.5% YoY INR; +27.5% YoY and +4.4% QoQ in USD)**, the fifth straight quarter above 5% QoQ INR growth and H1 USD growth at a four-year high. $1M+ clients rose by 5 to **59**. The Q1 promise was kept: **EBITDA margin held at 16.7%** (Q1 16.9%) through the annual wage hike, which historically pulls Q2 down. But **PAT is still not compounding**: ₹112 cr (+10% YoY, −3% QoQ). TTM PAT is **−8%** while TTM revenue is **+36%**. Other income halved QoQ (₹29 → ₹15 cr) and D&A is double last year's (acquisition amortisation). The whole thesis now rests on management's H2 call: **EBITDA back to 19–20% from Q3**, plus *"EBITDA to grow faster than revenue… PAT margin [to] expand faster than EBITDA margin."*

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **The growth is real and broad.** Top-10 clients +13.5% QoQ, $1M+ clients 59 (from 28 in FY22), revenue per employee $79.6K (*"the highest among peers"*). GenAI and upstream deal wins. Part of the YoY figure is still inorganic (BioPharm, ~1 year since acquisition).
2. **The margin did not break, but has not recovered yet.** 16.7% vs 17.6% a year ago and the 19–20% historical band. The deck bridge shows the wage hike offset by workforce transformation, outcome-based deals and FX.
3. **The H2 promise is specific and near-dated.** Q1 call (CEO): *"Through the inflection point in Q3 FY27, the EBITDA in H2 FY27 will be back in the range… 19% to 20%."* CFO (Q2): wage hike now *"fully reflected in our cost base."* A ~300bp step-up in one quarter is a big ask.

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth drivers:** Enterprise commercial and medical solutions for top-20 pharma; GenAI content and operations (Content Super App agents); BioPharm data and targeting (promotional exposure 3× since acquisition); a GenAI engagement whose costs are already in the P&L and whose revenue starts in Q3 (Q1 call: *"most of that revenue will flow straight to the bottom line"*).
- **Re-rating triggers:** Q3 EBITDA ≥ 18.5% on the way to 19–20%; disclosure of organic growth; PAT growth catching up with revenue.
- **Key risks / de-rating:** Margin promise missed a third time (the 150bp investment drag dates back to Aug 2025); AI deflation of content and operations pricing; client concentration in top-20 pharma; pharma budget cuts (US pricing reforms). **Severity: Medium.**
- **Abnormal / standout:** Q2 margin held despite wage hikes, *"a marked improvement on historical Q2 margin trends."*
- **Catalysts:** Q3 margin (Jan); the Q2 transcript on organic growth; amortisation roll-off.
- **Expectations read:** At 35× TTM, near its 52-week high and +5.5% on the day after results, the market has already priced H2 margin delivery. **Ahead** of the reported numbers.
