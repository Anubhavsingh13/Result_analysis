# Onward Technologies — Q2FY27 Result & Concall Analysis
**Quarter:** Q2FY27 (Jul–Sep 2026) · **Results/Call:** 16 Oct 2026 (transcript filed 26 Oct) · **Basis:** Consolidated
**Speaker:** Jigar Mehta (MD) · Host: Go India Advisors
**Sources:** BSE press release, earnings presentation and transcript (Q2); Q1FY27 transcript (16 Jul 2026); Screener.in quarterly series. CMP ₹386 (28 Oct, −5% on the day), M-cap ₹858 cr, TTM P/E 17.2×, 52-wk ₹208–496, dividend yield 2.1%.
*First Onward file in the concall archive (baseline).*

## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🟢 Thesis Strengthened**
**Management Credibility: B+** (guidance raised on delivery; the one-off was disclosed voluntarily; it is a single-spokesperson call)
**Results Verdict:** Print **Strong** · Quality **Medium** (one-off credit, rising DSO) · Trajectory **Accelerating**

The best quarter in the company's history. Revenue from operations was **₹170.2 cr (+22.4% YoY, +13.9% QoQ; +17.7% YoY / +13.5% QoQ in constant currency)**, and FY27 guidance was **raised to 15–18% revenue growth with a 13.8–14.3% EBITDA margin** (previously "double-digit"). Strip out the one-off, though. Reported EBITDA (₹26.6 cr, **15.6%**) includes a **₹2.9 cr UK R&D credit relating to FY26**. Underlying EBITDA is ~₹23.7 cr (**~13.9%**): +160bp QoQ, but roughly −40bp YoY. Underlying PAT growth is ~+23%, not +41%. The real story is volume: +432 net engineers in H1, a 3-year renewal up 72%, offshore delivery at ~71%, and data-centre engineering demand from US industrial clients. DSO rose to 80 days (+7) on unbilled build-up, which management says normalises in H2.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **Growth step-up is real and mostly constant-currency.** +17.7% CC YoY, +13.5% CC QoQ. MD: *"revenue is not the challenge. For a company of our size, execution is the challenge."*
2. **The margin headline is flattered.** Analyst: *"even if we exclude the R&D credit INR2.9 crores, the margin still expanded sequentially."* MD: *"This is not for this quarter. This is for FY26."* Underlying 13.9% is in line with the new 13.8–14.3% FY guide, so the guide does not assume repeat credits.
3. **Guidance looks conservative on its own maths.** FY26 revenue ~₹544 cr → FY27 at 15–18% = ₹626–642 cr. H1 was ₹319 cr, so H2 needs only ₹307–323 cr, *below* Q2's run-rate × 2 (₹340 cr). Either sandbagging or caution on US immigration rules (*"the guidance that we have given is post the new regulatory environment"*).

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth drivers:** US/European industrial and heavy-machinery OEMs (construction/mining equipment, off-highway) ramping offshore; AI data-centre engineering (power, cooling, electrical); clients billing over $1 m up from 16 to 18; ₹44.5 cr 3-year renewal (+72% vs the prior term).
- **Re-rating triggers:** Delivering the top end (≥18%) with an underlying margin ≥ 14%; a large new logo or RFP win (*"participating in bids for several large RFPs"*); the Chennai AI lab (Q4/early 2027).
- **Key risks / de-rating:** Client concentration (top 25 = 87% of revenue, per Q1 call); US onsite and immigration rules (PERM); rising unbilled/DSO; hiring execution (design centres full, new Pune seats); small-cap liquidity. **Severity: Medium.**
- **Abnormal / standout:** First-ever upward guidance revision (*"a first for Onward Technologies"*). Net cash ₹97 cr after the Q1 buyback.
- **Catalysts:** Q3 (furlough season; hiring slows); unbilled converting (*"Q3 and Q4, things getting normalized"*); Q4 hiring of "a few hundred" net.
- **Expectations read:** At ~17× TTM and ~14× FY27E (PAT ~₹60 cr ex-credit), the market is **behind** a 15–18% grower with net cash and a 2% yield. The −5% move on 28 Oct is sentiment or liquidity, not fundamentals.
