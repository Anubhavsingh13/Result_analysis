## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🟢 Thesis Strengthened (business) — valuation question unchanged**
**Management Credibility: B+** (capacity delivered and expanded; an accounting change flatters the headline)
**Results Verdict:** Print **Very strong** · Quality **Medium** (depreciation-method change) · Trajectory **Accelerating**

Revenue crossed ₹200 cr for the first time: **₹207.4 cr (+87% YoY, +43% QoQ)**, with EBITDA **₹50.3 cr at 24.3%** (+93bp YoY). Two engines fired at once. **Skids: 1,475 units, ₹50 cr** (+54% QoQ), and the **core hose/assembly business, ~₹157 cr (+42% YoY, +39% QoQ)**, led by US/Europe and ports & terminals. Two things change the headline, one positive and one negative:
- **Reported PAT ₹36.6 cr (+157%) is flattered.** Aeroflex switched depreciation from WDV to SLM from 1 Apr 2026, and Q2 absorbed the full-H1 benefit (₹8.41 cr). On the **old method, Q2 PAT is ₹28.3 cr (+99% YoY, +51% QoQ)**. That is still excellent.
- **Next-phase capex announced:** hose 20 → 30 mn m and skids 15,000 → **30,000/yr by Mar 2028**, ₹202 cr, management estimates "~₹500–550 cr incremental peak revenue". The company is also now an **approved supplier to a leading global digital-infrastructure / thermal-management provider**, a first step away from single-channel skid concentration.

The business is outrunning the 9 Oct stock report. Its FY28E base EPS (~₹8.7) is already matched by Q2's annualised like-for-like run-rate (₹8.6, WDV). At ₹542 the stock still trades at ~63× that run-rate. **Re-run the stock report**: the estimates are stale, but so is the case for paying 60×+.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **Both engines accelerated.** Skids went 46 → 571 → 1,040 → **1,475** units over four quarters (avg ₹3.39 lakh, up from ₹3.11 lakh). Core non-skid revenue jumped ~₹113 cr → ~₹157 cr QoQ. Hose capacity reached 20 mn m (Q2, ahead of the Q3 plan).
2. **Read PAT like-for-like.** Deck: *"Due to this change, depreciation expense for Q2 FY27 and H1 FY27 is lower by Rs 8.41 crs."* Q2 reported depreciation is effectively ~₹0.45 cr (the H1 SLM charge of ₹8.29 cr less the ₹7.84 cr already booked in Q1). Annualised, the change adds ~₹17 cr (~15–20%) to PAT. It is permitted under Ind AS 8, but it arrived in the same quarter the stock re-rated.
3. **Capacity doubling plus a global partner de-risks the skid story.** Skids 12,000 (done) → 15,000 by **Dec 2026** (Q1 had said Oct–Nov) → 30,000 by Mar 2028. ₹202 cr capex against ~₹500–550 cr incremental peak revenue is ~2.5× asset turns. The new global approval addresses the stock report's main risk (*"runs through a single Indian channel partner today"*).

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth drivers:** AI data-centre liquid cooling (India 9+ GW pipeline; now a global channel); core hoses in US/Europe (Americas +58%, Europe +82% YoY); ports & terminals; capacity +50% (hose) and +100% (skids) by FY28.
- **Re-rating triggers:** First export skid orders through the global partner; skid volumes above 2,000/quarter; EBITDA ≥ 25% (the "next few years" target, now 24.3%).
- **Key risks / de-rating:** Accounting-flattered PAT; working capital (receivables ₹130 → ₹204 cr in six months; H1 CFO only 0.69× PAT); skid price variability (₹1.1–5.5 lakh per unit range); entry of Parker/Senior; valuation (80× TTM, 14× book). **Severity: Medium (valuation High).**
- **Abnormal / standout:** +43% QoQ revenue at a stable margin. Core business +39% QoQ is unusually fast for a hose maker and warrants checking on the call for lumpy orders.
- **Catalysts (next 1–2 qtrs):** Q2 transcript (skid order book, global partner scope, core growth drivers); 15,000 skid capacity by Dec; Hyd-Air merger approvals; CFO handover (28 Nov).
- **Expectations read:** Earnings are catching up with the price faster than modelled, but at ~63× annualised like-for-like EPS the price still discounts the FY28 capacity fully utilised at today's margins.

*Growth read:* Compounding, and returns confirm it. ROCE is back near 30% as new capacity fills; the SLM change lifts reported ROE by ~2–3pp. This is the rare small-cap where revenue, margin and returns are all rising together.

**Margin walk (+124bp QoQ):** Higher-value products (skids, assemblies) and operating leverage on employee and other costs. Gross margin dipped ~150bp (material cost incl. inventory change 57.0% of revenue vs 55.5% in Q1), so the whole margin gain came from operating leverage (employee and other costs 18.7% vs 21.5% of revenue).
**Quality (Medium):** The depreciation-method change inflates reported PAT. H1 CFO ₹38 cr vs PAT ₹55 cr (0.69×); working capital absorbed ₹29 cr (receivables +₹74 cr since Mar; payables +₹56 cr). Short-term borrowings rose slightly to ~₹24 cr; the company is essentially debt-free. Tax normal at 26%.
**Narrative vs numbers:** The deck discloses the depreciation effect clearly (with an illustrative table), but the headline (*"PAT +157%"*) uses the flattered figure. The claim of *"improved working capital days"* holds on debtor days (~90 vs ~94 at Mar) because revenue rose faster than receivables, but cash conversion is still below 1×.

- 🟡 **Cash conversion 0.69×** and receivables +57% in six months: Low–Med (growth-driven; watch H2).
- 🟡 **CFO change** effective 28 Nov (internal re-designation): Low.
- 🟡 **Skid price dispersion** (₹1.1–5.5 lakh/unit): revenue per skid can swing with mix. Low.
