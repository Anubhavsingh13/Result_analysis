# Cyient Ltd — Q2FY27 Result Analysis
**Quarter:** Q2FY27 (Jul–Sep 2026) · **Results/Call:** 22 Oct 2026 · **Basis:** Consolidated (DET + DLM + Semiconductors)
**Sources:** BSE press release and Q2 investor presentation (22 Oct); management-change filings (6 Oct, 17 Oct); prior archive report `Q1FY27/CYIENT_Q1FY27_concall-analysis.md`; stock report `CYIENT_CyientLtd_2026-08-26.md` (Watchlist, 5.5/10); Screener.in quarterly series. ## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🔵 Thesis Intact (upgraded from 🟡 Watch)**
**Management Credibility: B** (unchanged; the margin target now looks on track, but the TAO run-rate disappoints)
**Results Verdict:** Print **In-line/Good** · Quality **Medium** · Trajectory **Bottomed, early recovery**

The two things Q1 asked for both moved the right way, if only just. **DET organic CC growth turned positive** (+0.4% YoY, +1.6% QoQ, after −0.9% in Q1), and **DET EBIT reached 14.2%** (+107bp QoQ, +206bp YoY, the highest in ten quarters), putting the 15% H1 FY28 target within reach. DET order intake rose **+20.8% YoY** ($158 m), the Strategic Units drag almost disappeared (−1% CC YoY vs −26%), and DLM's book-to-bill was 1.6×. **But the group P&L is still weak.** Normalised group PAT was ₹121 cr (**−4.8% YoY**). The semiconductor arm lost ₹45 cr at PAT, and debt more than doubled (gross ~₹2,870 cr vs ~₹1,440 cr) to fund TAO Digital. TAO itself is **shrinking** ($9.6 m, −21.5% YoY), well short of the "$40–50 m annual revenue" pitched at announcement. The core has bottomed; the bolt-ons are now the question.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **DET organic growth: positive, but only barely.** +0.4% YoY organic CC. Transportation & Mobility +16.5% YoY CC (sixth straight growth quarter) carries it; Networks & Infra −0.6%; Strategic Units −1%. One quarter of positive growth meets *half* of the stock report's buy trigger ("two quarters of positive DET CC growth").
2. **The margin target is finally credible.** DET EBIT 14.2% vs the 15% target for H1 FY28 (deferred twice). The Q2 lift came with positive growth, not cost cuts alone.
3. **The balance sheet turned.** Goodwill rose ₹2,455 cr → ₹4,241 cr and long-term borrowings ₹767 cr → ₹2,251 cr (TAO). DET net cash of ₹1,655 cr in Q1 is now a small net debt (~₹114 cr). Interest cost rose from ₹22 cr to ₹37 cr. TAO revenue is −21.5% YoY, so the acquired business is shrinking while it is paid for with debt.

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth drivers:** Transportation (aero, rail) +16.5%; DET order intake +20.8% with technology order intake +39.7%; DLM (aerospace, industrial, defence; book-to-bill 1.6×); semiconductors (ASIC pipeline >$100 m, custom ASIC order intake +50% QoQ, AI-DC power products from FY28).
- **Re-rating triggers:** A second quarter of positive DET organic CC growth (Q3); DET EBIT ≥ 14.5% sustained; semiconductor R&D turning into revenue; TAO stabilising.
- **Key risks / de-rating:** Semiconductor losses (*"memory shortages may slow near-term growth"*); acquisition-funded leverage; TAO shrinking; COO Prabhakar Atla's resignation (last day 28 Sep 2026); top-5 clients 32.8% of DET. **Severity: Medium.**
- **Abnormal / standout:** DET FCF ₹214 cr (139% of PAT); DSO movement turned positive after a Q1 build.
- **Catalysts:** Q2 transcript (TAO outlook, semiconductor breakeven timeline); Q3 DET organic growth; electronica 2026 product launches (Nov).
- **Expectations read:** At 31.5× TTM P/E on depressed group earnings (TTM PAT −34%), the price already assumes recovery. DET-only earnings support it better: DET PAT ₹153 cr/quarter.
