# Tata Elxsi — Q2FY27 Result & Concall Analysis
**Quarter:** Q2FY27 (Jul–Sep 2026) · **Results/Call:** 13 Oct 2026 (transcript filed 19 Oct) · **Basis:** Standalone (as reported; Tata Elxsi has no material subsidiaries)
**Speakers:** Manoj Raghavan (MD & CEO), Nitin Pai (CMO & CSO), Nalin Rana (CFO), Sneha V. (CS) · Host: EY
**Sources:** BSE Q2 transcript and earnings presentation/fact sheet; Q1FY27 transcript (Jul 2026) for promise tracking; Screener.in quarterly series. CMP ₹3,755 (28 Oct), M-cap ₹23,396 cr, TTM P/E 32.5×, 52-wk ₹2,996–5,950, dividend yield 2.0%.
*First Tata Elxsi file in the concall archive (baseline). No stock report in the library yet.*

## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🔵 Thesis Intact (tentative recovery)**
**Management Credibility: B** (candid margin bridges; growth aspirations keep running ahead of delivery)
**Results Verdict:** Print **In-line** · Quality **Medium** (margin gain is mostly FX) · Trajectory **Stabilising, early recovery**

For the first time in several quarters, growth was broad-based. Revenue grew **+2.3% QoQ / +7.8% YoY in constant currency** (₹1,055.7 cr, +15.0% YoY in INR), and all three verticals grew: Media & Communications +3.4%, Healthcare +3.5% (bottomed, as promised), Transportation +2.0%. The headline **EBITDA margin rose 70bp to 21.9%**, but the CFO attributed **60–70bp of it to cross-currency gains**. Removal of last quarter's one-offs (~150bp) was largely absorbed by the annual wage hike, so the underlying operating margin was roughly flat. PAT was ₹170 cr (flat QoQ, +10% YoY), and **TTM PAT is unchanged from a year ago** (₹670 cr) while the stock trades at 32.5×. Headcount fell again, to 11,149 (−6.7% YoY), with utilisation at 78% against an 81–82% peak. The business has stopped shrinking; it is not yet compounding.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **Growth is broad-based again, but Europe auto is still stuck.** CEO: *"Europe continues to be soft. There are large deals… but again with deal closures it's a wait and watch."* Growth came from US and India auto OEMs, new GenAI media deals and healthcare wins (home dialysis, Bayer, Terumo).
2. **Margin quality is weak.** CFO: *"about 60 bps to 70 bps was the cross-currency benefits… The balance, 10 bps to 20 bps is basically operational leverage."* The future margin levers named are utilisation (78% → ~82%) and *"correcting the pyramid"*, which is a 2–3 quarter, self-help story, not a deal-mix one (*"I would not expect that there is margin improvement that is going to follow simply because you won deals"*).
3. **Earnings have gone nowhere for three years.** Quarterly PAT ₹200 cr (Sep-23) → ₹170 cr (Sep-26); OPM 30% → 22%. At ~32× TTM the market still prices Tata Elxsi as a premium ER&D compounder. The recovery has to show up in margins, not just CC growth.

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth drivers:** Auto OEM deals (Asian OEM connected-vehicle platform; OEMs now ~78% of auto revenue, up from suppliers); media and communication deal ramps and GenAI (virtual-assistant deal with a global tech leader); healthcare diversifying beyond the US; aerospace and defence adjacencies (Sarla eVTOL, test rigs) on a small base; "Others" SDS (+74% YoY).
- **Re-rating triggers:** EBITDA margin back above 24% (pre-FY26 levels were 27–30%) through utilisation and pyramid; Europe auto large deals closing (*"It could happen in this quarter. It could happen in the next quarter"*); headcount growth resuming.
- **Key risks / de-rating:** JLR exposure (management is *"closely monitor[ing] the situation at JLR"*: layoffs and business stress); Q3 furloughs (short quarter); top-client concentration (top 5 ≈ 49% of revenue per the deck); talent cost pressure from GCCs (*"we need to work hard to retain our talent"*); FX reversal removing the margin tailwind. **Severity: Medium.**
- **Abnormal / standout:** Revenue +7.8% CC YoY on **6.7% fewer employees**. That is better productivity and utilisation, but it limits capacity to take on large ramps.
- **Catalysts (next 1–2 qtrs):** Q3 furlough impact; Europe deal closures; JLR spending direction; margin with FX neutral.
- **Expectations read:** **Ahead** of the numbers. 32.5× TTM P/E on flat TTM earnings needs a return to double-digit EPS growth. The stock is ~37% below its 52-week high, so some de-rating has already happened.

*Growth read:* Revenue is recovering in constant currency, but the margin reset (30% → 22%) means three years of zero EPS compounding. Returns are still excellent in absolute terms (ROCE ~30%, net cash, 2% yield), so this is a growth-and-margin problem, not a quality problem.

**Margin walk (+70bp QoQ, per CFO):** Cross-currency +60–70bp; Q1 one-offs removed (~+150bp, transition and retention costs) and operating leverage +10–20bp; offset by the company-wide wage hike. Underlying margin ex-FX: roughly flat.
**Quality (Medium):** FX-driven margin gain; other income falling (₹35 cr vs ₹49 cr YoY); tax rate up to 29%. Clean otherwise (no debt, ESOP dilution negligible).
**Narrative vs numbers:** The deck credits *"operational efficiencies"* for the margin expansion. The CFO's own bridge says FX did most of it. The call was more honest than the deck.

## 2. Guidance & Outlook
- **No numeric guidance** (as usual).
- **Directional:** *"H2 will be better than H1"* for all businesses except Europe auto; Q3 *"typically a short quarter"* with possible furloughs; *"slightly upward margin trajectory"* for the rest of the year; no more wage hikes this year.
- **Margin path:** gradual improvement over *"the next 2 to 3 quarters"* via utilisation and pyramid; *"We don't want to give a number."*
- **vs prior call:** Q1 said one-offs would reverse and healthcare would grow in FY27 (both delivered). The long-term growth aspiration is unchanged (*"we're not changing our aspirations"*).

## 3. Management Language Read
- **Candid on margins:** the CFO quantified FX and one-offs both quarters, a strong candour signal.
- **Hedged on growth timing:** Europe deals *"could happen in this quarter. It could happen in the next quarter"*; aerospace growth undisclosed (*"it'll be misleading if I say we grew x percentage"*). Reasonable, but it leaves the H2 path qualitative.
- **Admitted structural issues:** pyramid distortion *"over the last… four to six quarters"* from bringing in senior talent to win deals; salary per employee outpacing revenue per employee. Owning this is a positive.
→ **B.**

## 9. Bottom Line
**🔵 Thesis Intact (tentative recovery) · Credibility B.** Growth is broad-based again and last quarter's promises (one-offs gone, healthcare growing) were kept. But the margin improvement is mostly FX, earnings are flat over twelve months and over three years, and Europe auto remains stuck. Still priced as a premium compounder (32.5×). Needs proof from margins, not just revenue.
**Watch next call (Jan):** Q3 CC growth through furloughs (≥ 1%); EBITDA ≥ 22% *ex-FX*; utilisation moving toward 80%; Europe large-deal closures; any change in JLR spending.
