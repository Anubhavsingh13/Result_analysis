# L&T Technology Services — Q2FY27 Result & Concall Analysis
**Quarter:** Q2FY27 (Jul–Sep 2026) · **Call:** 19 Oct 2026 (transcript filed 25 Oct) · **Basis:** Consolidated
**Speakers:** Amit Chadha (CEO & MD), Alind Saxena (ED & President), Rajeev Gupta (ED & CFO), Munjay Singh (COO), Sandesh Naik (IR)
**Sources:** BSE Q2 transcript and investor presentation; Q1FY27 transcript (14 Jul 2026) for promise tracking; Screener.in quarterly series. CMP ₹4,048 (28 Oct), M-cap ₹42,947 cr, TTM P/E 30.7× (inflated by a one-off gain), 52-wk ₹3,010–4,747.
*First LTTS file in the concall archive (baseline).*

## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🔵 Thesis Intact (improving)**
**Management Credibility: B** (near-term promises delivered early; the 13–15% CAGR aspiration still runs ~2–3× ahead of actual growth)
**Results Verdict:** Print **In-line/Good** · Quality **Medium** (one-off gain, FX) · Trajectory **Gradually accelerating**

A solid execution quarter. CC revenue grew **+2.7% QoQ / +4.8% YoY**, EBIT margin rose to **16.3%** (+60bp QoQ, +220bp YoY), large-deal TCV doubled to **$203 m** (the Q1 slippage closed), and YTD FCF was 108% of net income. Read the headline PAT carefully. ₹549 cr (+67% YoY) includes a **₹165 cr one-time gain** on the Smart World & Communication (SWC) divestment; ex-gain PAT is ~₹384 cr (+17% YoY). INR revenue growth of +12.1% is helped by ~9% rupee depreciation. Management calls the "mid-16%" margin aspiration "achieved ahead of time". At 16.3%, that is generous but close. Growth is improving but still far from the **13–15% CAGR** aspiration the company keeps repeating.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **Margins are delivering ahead of schedule.** Q1 call: *"mid-16% EBIT margin on or before Q4 FY27."* Q2: 16.3%, with expansion in all three segments (Mobility 16.9%, Sustainability 29.5%, Tech 12.0%).
2. **Deal momentum is back.** Large-deal TCV went from ~$100 m in Q1 (*"a few large deal wins… moved to early part of Q2"*) to **$203 m**, over 50% AI-led, and about half in Sustainability (the most profitable segment, +13.4% YoY).
3. **The H2 promise is now explicit.** CEO: *"H2 will be better year-on-year and quarter-to-quarter than H1."* There is still no FY revenue number; the long-term aspiration is 13–15% CAGR against ~5% CC today.

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth drivers:** Sustainability (data centres, energy transition, plant engineering; 30% of revenue). Mobility recovering (Auto NA, aero, rail, trucks). A new **data-centre engineering business line**. A large AI/DC programme with a global telecom equipment maker ramping. EI (AI) revenue at a ~$120 m annual run-rate.
- **Re-rating triggers:** Two to three quarters of 3%+ QoQ CC growth with a ~16.5% margin; Auto Europe recovering (*"should bounce back next year"*); more $50 m+ deals.
- **Key risks / de-rating:** MedTech programme ramp-down (next phase only in H2); pure-play software clients hit by AI tools; Q3 furloughs; Auto Europe muted. **Severity: Medium.**
- **Abnormal / standout:** ₹165 cr divestment gain. Offshore mix 55.9% and fixed-price mix 35.6%, both rising (margin levers).
- **Catalysts:** Q3 furlough impact; Nokia engineering collaboration (26 Oct release); large-deal ramp-ups; first full quarter without SWC.
- **Expectations read:** Roughly in line. ~28× normalised annualised EPS (₹143) prices ~10–12% growth. That needs the H2 acceleration to show.

## 2. Guidance & Outlook
- **Hard:** EBIT to *"maintain this margin range"* (~mid-16%) near term; ETR 26–26.5%; DSO 80–85 days (now 78).
- **Directional:** H2 > H1 both QoQ and YoY; Sustainability double-digit for FY27; Auto Europe *"should bounce back next year."*
- **Long-term aspiration:** 13–15% revenue CAGR with 16–17% EBIT over 3–5 years (reiterated).
- **vs prior call:** Margin target pulled forward (achieved ~2 quarters early). Still no FY revenue guidance.

## 3. Management Language Read
- **Confident, mostly specific:** segment margins, deal TCV, DSO, ETR all quantified.
- **Generous framing:** 16.3% presented as having achieved a "mid-16%" aspiration.
- **Deflection:** asked for an FY growth number: *"we are not providing quarter-on-quarter on annual guidance. What I'm confirming is that H2 will be better than H1."*
- **Repetition risk:** the 13–15% CAGR aspiration appears in every call while CC growth sits at ~2–5%. That is the credibility gap to watch.
