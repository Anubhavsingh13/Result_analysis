## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🔵 Thesis Intact (recovery holding; quarter-on-quarter momentum slowing)**
**Management Credibility: B** (guidance beaten; project timelines on track)
**Results Verdict:** Print **Strong YoY / flat QoQ** · Quality **Medium-High** · Trajectory **Plateauing at a higher level**

Another record quarter: revenue **₹795 cr (+40% YoY)**, EBITDA **₹114.9 cr (14.4%, +321bp YoY)**, PAT **₹65 cr (+117% YoY)**. H1 revenue is +38.6% against **FY27 guidance of 15–20%**. The diversification story is moving: the **non-ibuprofen portfolio grew ~70% YoY and is now 45% of pharma revenue** (38% a year ago), and chemicals EBIT margin rose to ~7% (1% a year ago). But the sequential picture is flat. Revenue +5% QoQ and PAT +2% QoQ; pharma revenue was flat QoQ (₹469.5 → ₹472.8 cr) and pharma EBIT fell slightly (₹68.8 → ₹68.1 cr). All of the QoQ growth came from chemicals. The recovery has held at a higher plateau but is no longer accelerating.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **Diversification is real.** Non-ibu ~45% of pharma (+70% YoY); Clopidogrel/China approval; CDMO formulations (1,500 mn tablets, EU-GMP) commercialising in Q3 FY27. JMD: *"forward integration into CDMO and formulation manufacturing is a natural extension of our process expertise."*
2. **Pharma has plateaued QoQ.** Segment revenue and EBIT were flat sequentially. Whether ibuprofen pricing is holding, or the non-ibu growth is offsetting ibu softness, needs the transcript.
3. **Guidance is obsolete.** H1 +38.6% vs a 15–20% FY guide (reiterated in Sep). H2 at the Q2 run-rate gives FY27 ~₹3,150 cr (+32%).

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth drivers:** Non-ibu APIs (paracetamol 10,800 MTPA, metformin, clopidogrel, pantoprazole); CDMO formulations (Q3 FY27); specialty-chemical tolling plant for a global chemical company (FY27); ibuprofen +6,000 MTPA (₹350 cr, Dec-2027), since current ibu capacity is *"exhausted up to the level of 90% to 95%"* (Sep call).
- **Re-rating triggers:** CDMO revenue visible in Q3–Q4; non-ibu > 50% of pharma; EBITDA ≥ 15% sustained. Management says the new projects add *"an incremental 25% to around 30% to our top line over the coming years."*
- **Key risks / de-rating:** Ibuprofen and ethyl-acetate price cycles (~68% of sales); Chinese capacity returning; a large ibu capex (₹350 cr) while *"some other players… are thinking of exiting that business"*, a classic capital-cycle tell; working capital (H1 cash-flow slide does not extract cleanly; verify CFO). **Severity: Medium.**
- **Abnormal / standout:** Chemicals EBIT ₹23 cr vs ₹5 cr YoY, a commodity-spread swing.
- **Catalysts:** Q2 transcript (ibu pricing, CDMO timeline); Q3 first CDMO revenue; tolling-plant start.
- **Expectations read:** ~26× TTM on recovering earnings. Cheaper than the 9 Oct stock report entry (₹201), but still well above its ₹115 buy-below. U/D is unlikely to have crossed 1.5:1.

## 4. Capital Allocation
- Heavy reinvestment: ibu expansion ₹350 cr, CDMO ~₹110 cr, tolling plant (customer-led), 100-acre land bank. Funded by internal accruals plus modest debt (interest ₹5 cr/quarter).
- **Capital-cycle flag:** expanding commodity ibuprofen by 50% while peers consider exiting, which works only if IOL stays the cost leader. The customer-led tolling and CDMO projects are the higher-quality uses.
