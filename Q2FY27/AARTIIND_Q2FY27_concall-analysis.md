## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🟢 Thesis Strengthened (operations); balance sheet still the constraint**
**Management Credibility: B (unchanged)**

This is the best clean operating quarter in about four years. Unlike Q1, there were no FX gains or inventory gains propping it up.
- **Volumes rose more than 30% YoY.** Energy volumes grew 57% YoY and 51% QoQ, as West Asia normalised and fuel-additive capacity rose to 360 KTPA. Non-energy volumes grew 19% YoY.
- **Headline EBITDA was ₹418 Cr, only +9% QoQ.** But Q1 had a ₹46 Cr FX gain inside EBITDA, while Q2 took a ₹13 Cr FX loss. **Excluding FX, EBITDA was ~₹431 Cr vs ~₹339 Cr, up ~27% QoQ and ~41% YoY.**
- **A further ₹41 Cr is only deferred.** It is a consolidation-level stock-reserve provision on inventory still in transit, so the profit is recognised on delivery. Standalone EBITDA was ₹459 Cr.
- **Tax normalised** at 17%, compared with negative rates through FY26. The quality of PAT is now much higher.
- **The run-rate has nearly reached the FY28 target.** Annualised underlying EBITDA is ~₹1,700–1,750 Cr, close to the FY28 lower bound of ₹1,800 Cr, before Zone IV contributes.

**What caps the verdict:**
1. **Energy is now 50% of revenue,** riding "high refining margins and robust gasoline-naphtha cracks". That is cyclical and partly a West Asia windfall.
2. **Cash and the balance sheet went the wrong way.** H1 OCF was ₹398 Cr against ₹512 Cr of capex. Net debt rose ~₹340 Cr to ~₹4,650 Cr, about 3.2× TTM EBITDA against the <2.5× target. **A ₹1,000 Cr equity raise (~6% dilution) was approved on 31-Oct.**
3. **ROCE is still ~9% TTM *(est.)*, below the ~11% WACC.**

**Estimates:**
- Raise FY27 EBITDA to ~₹1,650–1,750 Cr *(est.)*, from ~₹1,500 Cr implied in Q1.
- Model FY28 at the low end of ₹1,800–2,000 Cr, haircutting energy spreads.
- Model ~6% more shares from the equity raise.

**Stock call:** the operating trigger has fired, so the 10-Oct AVOID deserves a refresh. With EV at ~12× underlying annualised EBITDA and the dilution still to come, it is a re-look, not a buy, yet.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **The quarter was driven by volume, not price or inventory.** Volumes grew more than 30% YoY. Underlying EBITDA rose ~27% QoQ even as an FX gain of ₹46 Cr turned into a loss of ₹13 Cr. In Q1, management promised that lost inventory gains "could be potentially compensated because of the volume growth" (CEO, Q1 Q&A). That has been delivered.
2. **Margin % fell, but absolute gross profit rose.** OPM fell to 10.6% (company basis) from 14.5% in Q1, because higher raw-material prices were passed on. Gross margin % fell from ~35.8% to ~28.3% *(est.)*, yet **gross profit in ₹ rose 21% QoQ to ~₹1,037 Cr**. In management's words, "absolute EBITDA remained protected, keeping EBITDA margins suppressed". Judge this quarter in ₹, not %.
3. **The balance sheet is funding the growth.** Inventory rose ₹534 Cr and payables ₹572 Cr in H1. Borrowings reached ₹5,147 Cr (+₹226 Cr), and cash fell to ₹468 Cr from ₹583 Cr. Interest cover is 3.5×. The ₹1,000 Cr equity raise, described as "the subsequent deleveraging, if any" (Chairman, EGM), would get net debt/EBITDA to ~2.5×. That fixes leverage by diluting shareholders, not from cash generation.

## Key Investor Insights
**Growth drivers:**
- **Fuel additives.** Capacity rose from 290 to 360 KTPA. Demand is strong across the USA, EU, MENA and India, and the company is broadening into a multi-product fuel-additive portfolio. *High conviction near-term; cyclical.*
- **NCB value chain.** The reversal of China's VAT rebate and its "anti-involution" campaign are giving Indian producers a share and margin tailwind. NCB capacity expansion is "under consideration".
- **Zone IV Phase I** is commissioned (CaCl2, PEDA, part of the MPP). Further blocks follow in Q3–Q4FY27 and ramp up over 18–24 months.
- **New 10+ year supply contract** worth ~₹1,500 Cr cumulative (~₹150 Cr a year), starting FY29, with the customer financing the capex (<US$25 m). It is small, but capital-light and a new global customer.

**Re-rating triggers:**
1. ROCE crossing 11% as the ~₹2,200 Cr of CWIP (Zone IV) starts earning.
2. Net debt/EBITDA below 2.5× after the equity raise, with OCF covering capex as FY28 capex intensity "reduces significantly".
3. Non-energy EBITDA growth, with Polymers and PDA recovering in Q3.

**Key risks / de-rating:**
1. **Energy concentration.** Half of revenue depends on gasoline cracks, and feedstock availability is "emerging" as a challenge. *High severity.*
2. **Inventory losses if crude falls.** The working-capital and inventory build reverses into losses in a falling-price regime. Imports carry "1 to 1.5 months of inventory" (CEO, Q1).
3. **Equity-raise dilution** of ~6% at ~₹450.
4. **Polymers, PDA and fluoro remain weak.** PDA is hurt by "subdued US demand and competition from China".
5. Weak monsoon and El Niño could hit agro demand in H2.

**Abnormal / standout guidance:** nothing new in numbers; the FY28 ₹1,800–2,200 Cr target was reaffirmed. The standout is the gap between **run-rate and target**: underlying EBITDA is already ~95% of the FY28 lower bound, a year early, and is mostly energy-led.

**Catalysts to watch:**
- The Q2 transcript: the energy spread outlook, how much of Q2 was windfall, and the H2 run-rate.
- The size, price and timing of the equity raise.
- Q3FY27 (Jan/Feb-2027): commissioning of further Zone IV blocks, Augene JV commercial volumes, and whether Polymers recover.

**Expectations read:** **ahead** of what the 10-Oct report assumed. The stock has not moved (₹460 → ₹450), so the market is waiting on the dilution and doubts how durable energy is.

**Results Verdict — Print: Strong · Quality: Medium-High (clean on tax and FX; stock-reserve timing; energy-cycle exposure) · Trajectory: Accelerating.**

**Margin walk (QoQ, company OPM 14.5% → 10.6%):**
- **Pass-through effect.** Revenue rose ₹1,281 Cr against gross-profit growth of only ~₹182 Cr. Higher raw-material prices inflate the denominator, so the margin % falls while profit in ₹ rises.
- **FX swing −₹59 Cr:** from a ₹46 Cr gain in Q1 to a ₹13 Cr loss in Q2.
- **Stock-reserve provision −₹41 Cr.**
- **Offset:** volume operating leverage. Other expenses rose 23% and employee costs 3%, against volume growth of ~30–50%.

**Quality of results:**
- **Clean:**
  - other income ₹7 Cr (3% of PBT);
  - no exceptional items in Q2 (Q1 had +₹2 Cr);
  - tax rate 17%;
  - FX is a *drag*, not a help.
- **Timing:** the ₹41 Cr stock reserve should reverse when in-transit volumes are delivered, but it will recur while export voyages lengthen. DAP terms on US shipments with "2 to 3-month voyage time" (CEO, Q1) cause lumpiness.
- **Cash:**
  - H1 OCF ₹398 Cr (₹548 Cr in H1FY26), against operating profit before working-capital changes of ₹789 Cr;
  - working capital absorbed ₹266 Cr net (inventory −₹534 Cr, receivables −₹343 Cr, payables +₹611 Cr);
  - free cash after capex was **−₹114 Cr**, or **−₹278 Cr after ₹164 Cr of interest paid**.

**Narrative vs numbers:** the headline is "Higher Volumes… highest quarterly revenue and EBITDA", and it is broadly accurate. The release is candid that margins were "suppressed" by pass-through. It is silent on the cash shortfall and the rise in debt; the presentation only says "working capital and debt increased due to increase in input prices".

## 2. Guidance & Outlook

**Hard guidance:**
- **FY28 EBITDA ₹1,800–2,200 Cr, Debt/EBITDA <2.5×:** reaffirmed in the Q2 presentation.
- **FY27 capex ₹700–800 Cr** (Q1): H1 spend was ₹512 Cr, so on track. Capex intensity is "expected to reduce significantly" from FY28.
- **Zone IV:** Phase I commissioned in Sep-2026. Further blocks follow in Q3–Q4FY27, and all blocks are to be commercialised "during FY27", with the ramp-up "over next 18-24 months".
- **Other projects:**
  - Augene JV: ramp-up from H2FY27.
  - Circularity JV: commissioning in H2FY27.
  - DCB debottleneck to 140 KTPA: underway.
- **Equity raise:** up to ₹1,000 Cr enabling approval (special resolution, EGM 31-Oct).

**Soft guidance:**
- "AIL expects to sustain its growth momentum" (press release).
- Polymers are "expected to improve in Q3 FY27".
- Agro "weak monsoon might have impact in H2".
- Energy faces "emerging challenges in availability for adequate feedstock".

**vs prior call:**
- **FY28 target:** reaffirmed.
- **Augene JV:** commissioning "in Q2FY27" (Q1) has become "commissioning trial and ramp-up from H2 FY27", a slight slip.

**Run-rate check:** H1 company-basis EBITDA was ₹802 Cr. With H2 at roughly Q2's underlying ~₹430 Cr per quarter, FY27 lands at ~₹1,650–1,700 Cr. For FY28, ₹1,800 Cr needs only modest Zone IV and JV contributions, *provided* energy spreads hold.

## 4. Capital Allocation
- **Capex:** ₹512 Cr in H1 against a ₹700–800 Cr FY guide. CWIP is ₹2,222 Cr, ~16% of assets, and earns nothing yet. The sustained ROCE below WACC (FY23–26) comes from this capex wave.
- **Debt:**
  - borrowings ₹5,147 Cr (₹4,921 Cr in March), of which ₹3,142 Cr is short-term;
  - net debt ~₹4,650 Cr *(est.)*, about 0.74× net debt/equity;
  - ~3.2× TTM EBITDA.
- **Equity raise:** up to ₹1,000 Cr via QIP, preferential issue or private placement, roughly 6% dilution at the current price. It deleverages at shareholders' expense rather than from free cash flow. The positive reading is that it is a balance-sheet bridge to FY28, when capex falls.
- **Partnerships:**
  - The customer-financed 10-year contract is an asset-light model and is accretive.
  - JVs (Augene, Re-Aarti) are equity-accounted. The auditor notes that Augene's interim financial information was not reviewed by its own auditor, and that it is immaterial.
- **Governance (minor):** Mrs. Ratanben Premji Gogri reclassified from promoter group to public; BSE and NSE gave no-objection on 7-Oct. It appears routine; check the size of her stake.
