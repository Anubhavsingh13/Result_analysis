## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🟡 Watch — Mixed Signals (leaning weaker)**
**Management Credibility: B−** (provisional until the transcript is out; timing optimism keeps showing up)
**Results Verdict:** Print **In-line** · Quality **High** · Trajectory **Stable top line / margins slipping**

The headline +25% revenue / +18% EBITDA / +10% PAT is mostly **recovery off a depressed Q2FY26 base**. Q2FY27 revenue of ₹309 cr is *flat* against Q2FY25's ₹310 cr, and PAT of ₹70 cr is still **27% below** Q2FY25's ₹96 cr. TTM revenue grew only +4%, and TTM PAT is −13%. More important for the thesis: the **margin bridge to 40% went backwards**. Gross margin fell 400bp (input costs and mix), EBITDA margin was 33.8% (−200bp YoY), and management's Q1 promise that EBITDA would grow faster than sales was missed (+18% vs +25%). Export growth slowed sharply, from +46% in Q1 to **+8%** in Q2. On the positive side, cash conversion is clean (H1 OCF ₹168 cr = 1.3× PAT), the balance sheet is debt-free, and a USFDA inspection closed with one minor procedural observation. Estimates should be trimmed on margin. The 40% EBITDA in FY28 now looks like FY29.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **The margin-recovery thesis slipped a quarter.** Q2 EBITDA margin 33.8% vs 35.8% YoY. GM 76.2% vs 80.2%. Deck: *"Margins were lower on account of higher input costs & change in product mix."* In Q1 the CEO had attributed GM to *"strong pricing discipline… and limited competitive intensity"*. Ex-injectables and Stellon, margin was 37.1%, still short of 40%.
2. **Growth mix is domestic, not export.** Domestic +42% (₹179 cr) vs exports +8% (₹130 cr). Export is the high-value regulated-market API franchise. After Q1's +46% export surge, Q2 suggests Q1 was partly order timing. API +30%, formulations +6% (H1 formulations −8%).
3. **Quality of earnings is good.** H1 OCF ₹168 cr vs PAT ₹127 cr, working capital released ₹27 cr, zero debt. Treasury plus investments plus cash is about ₹450 cr. But the inventory unwind promised for H1FY27 **did not show up**: on our reading of the deck, inventories went ₹326 cr → ~₹398 cr (verify against the results filing).

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth drivers:** Wallet-share gains and new products at existing customers. 2 new ANDAs (Tofacitinib, MMF OS). Inspira dermatology launch (domestic branded). Injectable plant (~₹600 cr revenue capacity, ~5% utilised in Q1). CDMO still only 1–2% of sales.
- **Re-rating triggers:** Injectable third-party contracts commercialising in H2FY27 (Q1 guidance). Margins back above 36% for two quarters (the stock report's trigger). A large innovator/CDMO contract.
- **Key risks / de-rating:** Input-cost inflation not passed through (GM −400bp). Export slowdown (US generic-tariff overhang flagged on the Q1 call). Every year the 40% margin slips costs ~₹200–250/share (pre-bonus) of value (stock report). **Severity: Medium.**
- **Abnormal / standout:** None positive. The standout negative is GM compression in a business that claimed pricing power one quarter ago.
- **Catalysts (next 1–2 qtrs):** Q2 concall: injectable contract status, export outlook, RM-cost pass-through. Q3 margin. Bonus credit (Nov). Any US tariff action on generics/APIs.
- **Expectations read:** At ₹1,311 (48× TTM), the price still embeds ~18–19% revenue CAGR with ~40% margins (stock report PIE). This print is **behind** that. The stock report's negative trigger ("Q2/Q3 growth <15%") was **not** hit (+25%), but the positive trigger (≥36% margin) wasn't either. That leaves it on the Watchlist; the ₹1,050–1,150 buy zone stands.
