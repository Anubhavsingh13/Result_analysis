## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🟢 Thesis Strengthened, but with an asterisk on durability**
**Management Credibility: A− (Underwritable; tends to guide conservatively)**

This is the margin inflection the July stock report was waiting for, and it is now two quarters old. **EBITDA per tonne was ₹26,920 in Q2.** That is **+56% YoY** and well above the ₹21–22k management implied for Q2–Q4 on the Q1 call. Volumes grew high single digits, in line with the 6–8% guide. PAT rose 80% on a clean P&L: no exceptional items, a normal tax rate and negligible other income. H1 already gives ~₹30.9k/t *(est.)*. Even if H2 runs at only the guided ₹21–22k, the **full-year per-tonne guide of ₹24–25k looks beatable**, so expect it to be raised. The asterisk is that part of the per-tonne gain comes from West Asia supply shortages, which pushed finished-product prices up. Management admitted in Q1 that this will normalise. Two other things need watching: **Specialty Care volumes were flat** even though the headline speaks of a "richer specialty mix", and working capital absorbed almost all of H1's profit. **Estimates:** raise FY27 EBITDA to ~₹750–800 Cr *(est.)*, roughly ₹26–27k/t on ~290k t, from ~₹690 Cr at the top of guidance. Keep a normalised **₹22–23k/t** for FY28. At ~19× TTM earnings and ~20× normalised FY28 *(est.)*, the price is fair for a franchise with these returns, not cheap. The stock is already above the July report's ₹1,600–1,800 accumulation zone, so this is a hold/accumulate-on-dips name, not one to chase.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **The per-tonne beat is large and real.** EBITDA per tonne was ₹26,920, against ₹21–22k guided ("for the next three quarters, INR 21,000 to INR 22,000", MD, Q1 Q&A) and ₹17.3k a year ago. Gross margin per tonne rose ~42% YoY *(est.)*, from about ₹48k to ₹68.5k. Some of that went to freight and other costs, which rose ~₹8k/t on doubled export freight. The ₹8k/t sequential fall from Q1's ₹35k is normalisation, not deterioration.
2. **Most of the 36% revenue growth is price, not volume.** Volume grew ~8% and realisation per tonne ~26% *(est.)*, while fatty alcohol was flat YoY ($2,763/t vs $2,800/t). The price gain came from freight pass-through on CIF exports, a weaker rupee, shifts in product mix, and supply-shortage pricing caused by West Asia. The last of these is cyclical and will fade.
3. **The growth engine has shifted.** Performance Surfactants (the commodity side) grew volumes in the mid-teens, while **Specialty Care was flat**. Rest of the World (Europe, APAC, Americas) volumes fell by a high single digit. India and AMET (Africa, Middle East and Turkey) each grew in the mid-teens. Specialty's share of revenue *fell* from 37% to 35% YoY. "Richer specialty mix" holds only *within* specialty (premium and masstige products), not for the portfolio as a whole.

## Key Investor Insights
**Growth drivers:**
- India volumes grew in the mid-teens across Tier-1, non-Tier-1 and D2C customers. Rural demand and festive demand are the swing factors for H2.
- AMET has recovered: up mid-teens YoY after −4% in Q1. Egypt is back to normal.
- Reformulation headwinds have reversed. "Gave us the volumes back and also good operating leverage" (MD, Q1).
- *High conviction on India; moderate conviction on AMET (geopolitical exposure).*

**Re-rating triggers:**
1. An FY27 per-tonne guidance upgrade at the Q2 call, which is likely given H1 at ~₹30.9k.
2. A steady-state per-tonne figure of ≥₹22k for FY28, versus the ₹19–21k structural band. That would justify a higher normalised EPS of roughly ₹110–120 *(est.)*.
3. The inorganic specialty deal management has been signalling: "M&A… huge amount of work is on" (MD, Q1).

**Key risks / de-rating:**
1. **Normalisation of windfall spreads** as West Asia supply recovers. Every ₹1k/t is about ₹29 Cr of EBITDA. *Medium-high severity.*
2. **Specialty stalling:** flat volumes, and ROW down a high single digit. Specialty is the long-term multiple driver.
3. **Working capital:** receivables rose from ₹750 Cr to ₹1,113 Cr (+48% in six months). H1 OCF was ₹77 Cr against ₹448 Cr EBITDA.
4. A pending GIDC land case (₹72.5 Cr carrying value). The district court has ruled in Galaxy's favour and the High Court stay remains.

**Abnormal / standout guidance:** in Q1, management raised the FY27 per-tonne guide by about ₹4.5k (from ₹19–21k to ₹24–25k) while keeping the volume guide unchanged. Q2 has already beaten the implied run-rate, so the next call is likely to bring a second raise. This management habitually under-promises. The volume guide for Q1 at the "higher end of 6–8%" did slip to 5.5%.

**Catalysts to watch:**
- The Q2 transcript: whether the per-tonne guide is revised, and the H2 run-rate.
- Q3FY27 (Feb-2027): per-tonne figure at or above ₹22k, Specialty Care volume growth, and whether receivables unwind.
- Any announcement on specialty M&A.

**Expectations read:** **ahead** of what was priced in July. The stock is up ~13% since then and near its ₹2,697 high's range. The market is pricing a partial upgrade. Further upside needs proof that ₹22k+/t is the new floor.

**Per-tonne walk (YoY, ~₹+9.7k/t; volumes ~67.4k t → ~72.8k t, all *est.*):**
- Gross margin per tonne: ~₹48.2k → ~₹68.5k (**+₹20.3k**). Gross margin % rose from 24.5% to 27.5%.
- Other expenses per tonne: ~₹19.9k → ~₹28.1k (**−₹8.2k**), mostly freight.
- Employees per tonne: ~₹11.9k → ~₹14.4k (**−₹2.5k**); employee cost +30% YoY.
- Net result: **~+₹9.6k/t.** The spread gain is large. Part of it is freight that was passed through in price and paid out again in cost.

**Quality of results:**
- **P&L: clean.** No exceptional items (FY26 had a ₹11.9 Cr labour-code charge). Tax rate is a normal 21%. Other income is 4% of PBT.
- **Cash: weak.** H1 OCF was ₹77 Cr, against ₹169 Cr in H1FY26 and ₹442 Cr of operating profit before working-capital changes.
  - Receivables: ₹750 → ₹1,113 Cr.
  - Inventory: ₹794 → ₹1,024 Cr.
  - Payables: ₹651 → ₹856 Cr.
  - Receivable days ~65 vs 52 at FY26 *(est., TTM basis)*. Higher realisations explain part of this, not all.
  - Gross borrowings rose from ₹165 Cr to ₹230 Cr. Net cash slipped from ~₹580 Cr to ~₹520 Cr *(est.)* after the ₹78 Cr final dividend.

**Narrative vs numbers:**
- **"Volume-led growth"** (press-release headline): this holds for EBITDA but not for revenue, where volume is only about a quarter of the growth.
- **"Richer Specialty Care portfolio mix"** (headline): this is contradicted at the portfolio level. Specialty volume was flat and its revenue share fell from 37% to 35%. It holds only within specialty, for premium and masstige products.
- **"PAT for the first six months… surpassed the PAT… for the entire previous financial year"**: true, but the comparison base is a 3-year-low year.

## 2. Guidance & Outlook

**Hard guidance (from Q1FY27; Q2 update pending the transcript):**
- FY27 volume growth: **6–8%**. H1 is about 7% *(est.)*, so on track.
- FY27 EBITDA per tonne: **₹24–25k**, raised in Q1 from ₹19–21k. That implies ₹21–22k for each of Q2–Q4.
- FY27 capex: about ₹150 Cr ("same as last year"), with no new growth capex flagged.

**Soft guidance (Q2 press release):** "Demand indicators remain encouraging across key markets"; "remains vigilant regarding developments in West Asia… freight rates and feedstock availability."

**Run-rate check:** H1 EBITDA was ₹448 Cr on ~145k t, about ₹30.9k/t *(est.)*. To land at the guided FY average of ₹24.5k, H2 would need only ~₹18k/t, which is below even the old structural band. **The guide is conservative; expect a raise to ~₹26–27k/t.**

## 3. Management Language Read *(press release and presentation only; transcript pending)*
- **Candor (positive, from Q1):** the MD openly guided a normalisation from ₹35k to ₹21–22k: "it will reverse as the supply situation normalize" (analyst framing he did not dispute). He conceded that "H2 can be lower" if rural demand weakens. He refused to quantify the windfall split: "I would not want to specifically comment… it will only confuse." That is a mild deflection, but the guidance itself carries the honest message.
- **Q2 tone: self-congratulatory.** The release praises "exceptional efforts and collaboration of our teams" and dwells on the H1-vs-FY26 PAT "milestone". This is fair given the delivery, but the specialty-mix framing overstates the case (see Lens 1).
- **Disclosure: good.** EBITDA per tonne, volume bands by region and segment, and the fatty-alcohol price are all disclosed. Volumes are given only as bands ("mid-teens", "high single digit"), not tonnes, which is a slight step back in precision.
- → **Credibility A−.**

- **Balance sheet:** net cash of ~₹520 Cr *(est.)* despite the working-capital build. D/E is ~0.08×.
