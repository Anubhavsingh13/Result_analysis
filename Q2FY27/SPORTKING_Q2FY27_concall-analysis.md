# Sportking India — Q2FY27 Result Analysis
**Quarter:** Q2FY27 (Jul–Sep 2026) · **Results/Call:** 30 Oct 2026 · **Basis:** Standalone (no subsidiaries)
**Sources:** BSE results filing (incl. notes), press release and investor presentation (30 Oct); Q1FY27 transcript (3 Aug 2026); prior archive report `Q1FY27/SPORTKING_Q1FY27_concall-analysis.md`; Screener.in. CMP ₹189 (2 Nov), M-cap ₹2,402 cr, TTM P/E 10.5×, P/B 1.9×.
**⚠️ The Q2 call transcript is not yet filed** (audio only). The language and Q&A lenses are limited.

## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🟡 Watch — Mixed Signals (unchanged)**
**Management Credibility: B** (Odisha on schedule; profit flattered by an accounting-estimate change)
**Results Verdict:** Print **Very strong** · Quality **Medium** (spread-driven plus a depreciation tweak) · Trajectory **Cyclical upswing extended**

The best quarter in the company's history: revenue **₹781 cr (+24.5% YoY)**, EBITDA **₹155 cr at 19.8%** (+940bp), PAT **₹96.4 cr (+241%)**. The two Q1 cautions both still apply. **Volume is flat:** yarn sales 21,172 MT vs 20,949 MT (+1%) and production 21,296 vs 21,294 MT. All of the growth is realisation and spread (*"Healthy realisations, favourable spreads"*). **Profit is flattered:** from 1 Jul 2026 Sportking extended plant useful life from **7.5 to 10 years**, cutting Q2 depreciation by **₹8.0 cr** (depreciation ₹14 cr vs ₹24 cr in Q1). Excluding that, PAT is ~**₹90 cr** (still +218% YoY). The structural legs are on track: the **Odisha 1.5 lakh-spindle plant starts production in Q3 FY27**, and solar power is cutting energy cost. At 10.5× TTM the market is not paying for the peak, which is correct.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **Two quarters of peak spreads, not a re-rating.** Gross margin 31.9% (+779bp YoY); EBITDA 19.8% vs a 6–13% history. Q1 call: *"We witnessed better realization across our product portfolio, particularly in cotton yarns"*; *"our strategic buying approach enabled us to effectively manage input costs"* (inventory gains).
2. **An accounting-estimate change in a peak quarter.** Useful life 7.5 → 10 years (triple shift), prospective from 1 Jul: depreciation −₹8.0 cr/quarter, ~₹32 cr/year pre-tax (~₹24 cr post-tax, ~8–10% of TTM PAT). Allowed under Ind AS 8 and disclosed in the notes, but not in the press release.
3. **Odisha is the real swing factor.** ~₹1,000 cr, 1.5 lakh spindles (roughly +100% of capacity), mostly term-loan funded. First phase Q3 FY27; Q1 call targeted ~90% utilisation *"by March end"* and margins *"at least 300 to 400 basis points"* above the old plants, helped by state incentives. Volume growth arrives from Q4.

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth drivers:** Odisha capacity (volume doubles over FY27–28); state incentives; solar (energy cost −12–15%); exports 52% of H1 revenue (+11% YoY); India–UK FTA and EU FTA progress; Chinese buying of Indian yarn (Q1 call).
- **Re-rating triggers:** Odisha at 90% utilisation by Mar-27 with ≥ 15% margin through a normal spread; debt peaking and falling from FY28.
- **Key risks / de-rating:** Cotton-yarn spread mean reversion (PAT could halve at 12% OPM); leverage rising (borrowings ₹549 cr at Mar-26, plus most of ₹1,000 cr); interest and depreciation step-up from Q3; commodity inventory risk. **Severity: Medium–High (cyclical).**
- **Abnormal / standout:** PAT +241% at flat volume; depreciation down ₹10 cr QoQ.
- **Catalysts:** Odisha commissioning (Q3); Q3 spreads (new cotton crop); FTA implementation.
- **Expectations read:** **In line to cheap** at 10.5× TTM, if spreads hold. At normalised 12% OPM on doubled capacity, earnings would be roughly today's run-rate, so the market is already capitalising a mid-cycle outcome.
