# Unimech Aerospace & Manufacturing — Q2FY27 Result Analysis
**Quarter:** Q2FY27 (Jul–Sep 2026) · **Results:** 27 Oct 2026 · **Basis:** Consolidated (Hobel Bellows consolidated from 1 May 2026)
**Sources:** BSE press release & Q2/H1FY27 investor presentation; Screener.in quarterly series; Q1FY27 concall transcript (4 Aug 2026); prior archive report `Q1FY27/UNIMECH_Q1FY27_concall-analysis.md`. CMP ₹1,619 (28 Oct, −4.2%), M-cap ₹8,241 cr, TTM P/E 93.5×.
**⚠️ The Q2FY27 concall transcript is not yet available.** Lenses 3 and 5 are limited to the press release and deck. Re-run when the transcript is filed.

## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🟢 Thesis Strengthened** (up from 🟡 Watch in Q1)
**Management Credibility: B** (delivered on every Q1 promise that has come due; order-book presentation is promotional)
**Results Verdict:** Print **Strong** · Quality **Med–High** · Trajectory **Accelerating**

Revenue ₹123 cr (+98% YoY, +14% QoQ), EBITDA margin **39.8%**, PAT ₹31.8 cr (+103%). H1 revenue is already 96% of FY26. This quarter answers the two biggest Q1 worries. **(1) Organic growth is real.** Stripping out Hobel (~₹33 cr est. for a full quarter), the legacy business grew roughly **+45% YoY**. **(2) Lumpiness:** this is now the third straight strong quarter after the Dec-25 air-pocket. Strategically, the Honeywell 10-yr LTA and a ~US$43 mn Tier-1 award letter move Unimech from short-cycle tooling toward recurring "flying parts". That is the re-rating thesis. Two counterweights keep this short of a clean beat. First, the *confirmed* order book actually **fell QoQ to ₹266 cr** (from ₹280 cr); the headline "₹855 cr, 8× YoY" includes ₹589 cr of LTAs/award letters that are not firm POs. Second, at 93× TTM the stock (₹695 → ₹1,619 in a year) is well past the stock report's ₹700–750 entry zone.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **Margins beat guidance.** Q2 EBITDA 39.8% and H1 38.3% vs FY27 guidance of **"34%–35%"** (Q1 call, Aakash Jaiswal). Material and conversion cost was 30.6% of revenue, implying ~69% GM vs the 65% "sustainable" guide. The press release credits a *"high-margin order mix, and strong operating leverage"*.
2. **Read the order book carefully.** The deck states the ₹855 cr total is *"Rs. 266 Cr (Confirmed PO's) + LTA & Award Letter ~Rs. 589 Cr"*. Confirmed POs are +153% YoY (₹105 → ₹266 cr) but −5% QoQ, a book-to-bill of ~0.9. The LTAs (Honeywell 10-yr, FACC US$7.5 mn, Tier-1 US$43 mn) are high-quality visibility, but they are frameworks, not backlog.
3. **QoQ growth is mostly Hobel's extra month.** Q1 Hobel was ~₹22 cr for 2 months, so a full quarter is ~₹33 cr. Organic revenue went from ~₹86 cr to ~₹90 cr (~+5% QoQ). The sequential acceleration promised in Q1 holds, but the organic engine is growing steadily, not explosively. The step-up depends on semiconductors (*"sharp step-up anticipated in H2"*) and nuclear (₹87 cr executing from H2).

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth drivers:** Aero tooling (LEAP/P&W/RR programs). PCA "flying parts" (250 FAIs proved, **346 more by FY27-end**). Semiconductor OEM (20-month development, commercial revenue in H1, H2 step-up). Nuclear ₹87 cr. Hobel cross-sell. Saudi JV (Kanoo, ~US$30 mn total, first tranche done).
- **Re-rating triggers:** Serial-production conversion of the FAI pipeline. NADCAP special-process certification (targeted by FY27-end). Hobel AS9100 (Q4FY27). Conversion of the US$43 mn award letter into POs. CARE A/Stable rating.
- **Key risks / de-rating:** Working capital climbing to **160+ days** (guided). QIP of up to ₹750 cr for minimum public shareholding (dilution plus overhang; due within ~18 months). Customer/program concentration. US tariffs (FTWZ mitigant). Valuation at 93× TTM. **Severity: Medium (valuation is the main one).**
- **Abnormal / standout:** A 40% EBITDA margin on +98% revenue is well above guidance. Flag whether that reflects tooling mix (lumpy) or structural change.
- **Catalysts (next 1–2 qtrs):** Q2 call: FY27 revenue framing, semicon H2 sizing, LTA conversion timeline. Q3: nuclear execution revenue. QIP launch/terms. Associate (Dheya) losses.
- **Expectations read:** The price already discounts LTA conversion and sustained ~35%+ margins. The print is **ahead** of guidance but roughly **in line with** what a 93× multiple needs. Little margin of safety.

## 3. Management Language Read *(release/deck only, provisional)*
- **Promotional framing:** *"Consolidated Order Book at 8,546 Mn up 8-times YoY"* is the press-release headline. The deck's fine print separates confirmed POs (₹266 cr) from LTAs/award letters. The disclosure is honest, but the headline is not like-for-like.
- **Q1 call candor (credit):** management volunteered that Q4FY26 PAT was flattered by other income (*"on an operating basis, the quality of earnings this quarter is meaningfully stronger"*) and pre-announced rising working-capital days. Both are signs of a team that names problems early.
- **Disclosure limits:** declines segment margins (*"we will not want to disclose a margin separately for each business"*). That limits checking Hobel's margin contribution.
→ **B**, unchanged. Upgrade to B+/A if the call reconciles the order-book framing and gives organic growth explicitly.

## 4. Capital Allocation
- Saudi JV: first equity tranche ~US$4 mn (SAR 15.3 mn) done, of ~US$30 mn joint investment (51% Unimech). Automated special-process cell installation underway. Dheya associate (micro gas turbines).
- Hobel (₹450 cr all-cash) is integrated and contributing about a quarter of revenue at margins that appear accretive at the group level.
- **QIP of up to ₹750 cr** approved, primarily for minimum public shareholding (~18 months). It is a dilution and supply overhang, not a growth-capital need. Treasury is already depleted by Hobel (other income fell by half in Q1).
- Drift check: four new growth vectors (Hobel, Saudi, Dheya, semicon) at once. So far all are adjacent to precision engineering, so the drift is acceptable.
