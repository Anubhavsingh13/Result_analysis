## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🟠 Thesis Weakening (strong orders, but management has now formally cut FY27 margin expectations)**
**Management Credibility: B− (slipping from B)**

Demand is no longer the question. Order intake hit a record ₹1,198 Cr (+36% YoY), backlog is ₹2,446 Cr (+14%) and revenue grew 18%. The problem is margins. Management's own FY27 guidance is now **10–11% reported EBITDA margin**. That is **below FY26's 11.4%** and contradicts the Q4FY26 promise to "at least maintain or grow margins". The 15% target has been moved to a new **"adjusted, excluding loss-making European entities"** metric (14–15%), and the full group benefit has been pushed to FY28. Europe got worse during the quarter: its EBITDA loss roughly doubled from ~₹14 Cr in Q1 to ₹29 Cr in Q2. Profits did not convert to cash either. H1 operating cash flow was ₹41 Cr against ₹204 Cr EBITDA, and the debt repayment came out of the cash balance. **Estimates:** take FY27 EBITDA down to the guided ~₹400–420 Cr (10.5% on ~₹3,950 Cr revenue). Do not credit FY28 margin recovery until Europe's losses start shrinking. This supports the 14-Oct **AVOID** at ₹1,208, which still prices in a turnaround.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **The margin guidance cut is the real news.** Management guided "EBITDA margin 10–11%" for FY27, and "14–15%" only after excluding loss-making European entities. A quarter ago the 15% target was the "minimum" (CFO, Q1FY27 Q&A). Today the reported group number is guided below last year's level.
2. **Europe is getting worse, not better.** The loss-making European entities lost ₹43 Cr of EBITDA in H1. Of that, ₹29 Cr came in Q2 alone. Excluding them, group margin was 15.9% in Q2. So the core business is fine and the losses are concentrated, which is a fixable problem. But restructuring is ongoing with no date given, and the CFO who promised a "direct uplift" from fixing these units left the company on results day.
3. **Orders are strong and broad-based, but working capital is absorbing the cash.** PPT order intake grew 85% YoY and HET's grew 254%. But customer advances fell ₹102 Cr while the backlog rose ₹252 Cr. H1 OCF was only ₹41 Cr (about 20% of EBITDA). Net debt rose from ~₹147 Cr to ~₹250 Cr (net debt/EBITDA went from 0.4× to 0.6×) even though gross borrowings fell by ₹103 Cr.

## Key Investor Insights
**Growth drivers:** (a) PPT revenue +34% YoY and order intake +85% (mixing, filtration, drying), with pharma, CDMO and peptides "strong". (b) HET (nuclear, power, oil & gas) order intake reached ₹201 Cr, a 6-quarter high. (c) Non-traditional industries are now 44% of H1 order intake, up from 31% in FY24. The diversification is real. *High conviction on revenue; H2 is covered by the backlog.*
**Re-rating triggers:** (1) European losses shrinking in absolute ₹ terms (₹29 Cr per quarter is the bar to beat). (2) Refinancing and the intercompany-loan unwind (guided "within 12–18 months") cutting the ~₹29–30 Cr per quarter underlying finance cost. (3) India segment margin returning to 13%+.
**Key risks / de-rating:** (1) **India is stalling.** India segment revenue was +1.7% YoY and PBIT −21% (₹27 Cr vs ₹35 Cr). The high-return core is no longer offsetting the losses abroad. *High severity.* (2) The record backlog is lower-margin: material cost was 43.8% of sales, against 36.7% a year ago (+710 bps), reflecting a shift to projects and systems. (3) Leadership churn mid-restructuring: the Group CFO left early "owing to personal exigencies" (second CFO exit in about 21 months), the CEO International retired in August, and the Group CEO has been in the role only since May. (4) Valuation: 46× trailing EPS on a ~5% ROE.
**Abnormal / standout guidance:** This is the **first explicit numeric FY27 margin guidance**, and it comes in *below* the prior year. The **"adjusted EBITDA excl. loss-making European entities"** metric is also new. Introducing a flattering adjusted metric at the same moment the reported metric is cut is a classic reframe.
**Catalysts to watch:** the Q2 transcript (Europe restructuring cost and timeline, the H2 margin bridge, why India is stalling). Q3FY27 (Feb-2027): Europe loss below ₹29 Cr and OCF turning positive after the working-capital build. Refinancing announcement.
**Expectations read:** **Behind** what's priced in. On 14-Oct the reverse DCF needed a 13–15% margin plus 14–18% revenue CAGR. Management has now guided 10–11% for FY27. The ~9% price fall since then has only started to adjust to that.

## 2. Guidance & Outlook

**Hard guidance (new):**
- FY27 revenue growth: "Double digit".
- FY27 EBITDA margin: "10–11%", or "14–15%" on an adjusted basis that "excludes loss making European entities".
- Refinancing and debt reduction "within next 12–18 months".
- Group tax strategy "within next 18–24 months".
- Intercompany loan termination "within next 12–18 months".

**Soft guidance:**
- "FY28 is set to deliver stronger growth, higher margins and improved earnings."
- "Reduced European drag: restructuring benefits begin to show" (an FY28 driver).

**vs prior calls:**
- **Margin: cut.** Q4FY26 said "at least maintain or grow" (vs 11.4%). Q1FY27 called 15% "the minimum target" and said "you will directly see an uplift in EBITDA margin for the full group" (Poempner, Q1 Q&A). Q2FY27 guides 10–11% for FY27 and pushes the uplift to FY28.
- **Revenue: reaffirmed or raised.** H1 grew 17%.
- **Run-rate check:** H1 margin was 10.2%. Hitting the top of the guided band (11%) needs H2 at ~11.6%, which requires Europe's losses to shrink and backlog mix to improve. **The bottom of the band is more likely.**

## 3. Management Language Read *(presentation and filings only; transcript pending)*
- **Candor (positive):** management quantified the European drag for the first time (₹43 Cr EBITDA loss in H1) and named its cause: "Europe's chemical industry is going through a major reset". That is more specific than Q1, where the CFO spoke of "one, two, three units which are really underperforming".
- **Reframing (negative):** the reported margin is cut while a new adjusted metric that excludes the problem keeps the "15%" story alive. "Excl. loss-making entities" is a moving perimeter, so it needs watching every quarter.
- **Selective emphasis:** "Improving Flow-through" is the slide title even though PAT fell 7% YoY. The "internal accruals" claim does not match the cash-flow statement.
- **Deferral pattern:** in Q1 the MD said "let us give a few quarters of consistent results and then we can sit down and talk about margins". The answer, one quarter later, is a lower number plus an FY28 promise.
- → **Credibility grade: B−.** Management is candid on orders and debt mechanics, but on margins the target keeps moving.

## 4. Capital Allocation
- **Debt:** ₹100 Cr of net long-term borrowings repaid (including ~EUR 7 m) and ₹16 Cr short-term. Gross borrowings fell ₹835 → ₹732 Cr, but this was funded from cash. Net debt/EBITDA rose 0.4× → 0.6×. CRISIL rating maintained at AA−/Stable.
- **Pension:** unfunded pension liabilities are ₹309 Cr (₹330 Cr at March). This is debt-like and sits outside the "net debt" figure.
- **Capex:** ₹55–66 Cr in H1, roughly maintenance level plus the low-cost hub build-out (Poland and India for Europe).
- **M&A:** none new. The SEMCO/GMM Inox purchase price allocation is finalised with no change. Goodwill and intangibles are ₹850 Cr, 66% of equity. Europe's losses raise impairment risk.
- **Drift:** none. Restructuring and simplification is the right agenda, but execution is slow.

## 9. Bottom Line
**🟠 Thesis Weakening · Credibility B−.** GMM is now clearly two businesses: a ~16%-margin global core that is growing on a record order book, and a European drag that is getting worse. Management has finally put a number on both, but the number for FY27 is a cut, and the recovery has moved to FY28. With India stalling, cash not converting and the CFO gone, there is no reason to pay 46× trailing earnings for a turnaround that has slipped another year. **Keep the stock report's AVOID; the buy-below of ₹550–610 is unchanged.**
