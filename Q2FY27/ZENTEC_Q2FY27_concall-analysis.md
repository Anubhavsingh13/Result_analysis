## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🟢 Thesis Strengthened (execution) — order-book target still the open question**
**Management Credibility: B** (the H2 back-loading promised in Q1 is showing up; the order-book and revenue targets are still far away)
**Results Verdict:** Print **Very strong** · Quality **Medium-High** · Trajectory **Re-accelerating (lumpy)**

The record quarter that Q1 promised. Revenue **₹345 cr (+99% YoY, +144% QoQ)**, operational EBITDA **₹132 cr at 38.2%**, PAT **₹89.5 cr (+51% YoY)**. CMD: *"Zen has begun delivering the Fast Track Procurement orders received last year, ahead of the contractual timelines."* H1 operational EBITDA margin is **35.1%**, exactly the "mid-30s" FY27 guide, after Q1's 27%. Order inflow was **₹482 cr** (book-to-bill 1.4×), taking the order book to **₹1,376 cr** (equipment ₹995 cr, AMC ₹381 cr). **Two cautions.** First, one big quarter does not change the trailing picture: TTM revenue is only +4% and TTM PAT is ~−13% YoY (Screener basis). Second, the order book must reach **~₹2,500 cr by March after execution**, which needs roughly **₹1,800 cr of H2 inflow**. At 73× TTM, the stock prices that conversion.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **The execution engine works when orders exist.** Revenue went ₹142 → ₹345 cr QoQ with margins rebuilding through operating leverage (fixed costs ~₹70–75 cr/quarter): 27% → 38%.
2. **The order book grew, but slowly against the target.** ₹1,239 cr (Jun) + ₹482 cr inflow − ₹345 cr execution = **₹1,376 cr**. The stock report's "~₹1,711 cr tracked" was GST-inclusive and before Q2 execution. The FY27-end target of ~₹2,500 cr requires a large anti-drone and simulator tender wave in H2.
3. **The policy tailwind is real but unquantified.** The release cites the DAC's AoN approvals, *"around 82% … earmarked for sourcing from the domestic defence industry, including categories in which Zen has fielded products."* No specific tender wins are named.

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth drivers:** FTP anti-drone deliveries; simulator upgrades (tank gunnery ₹177.5 cr, Jul); exports (₹92 cr of the order book); AMC annuity (₹381 cr); subsidiaries in naval simulation, drone propulsion, RCWS and UGVs.
- **Re-rating triggers:** Large anti-drone or simulator RFP wins in H2 (needed for ₹2,500 cr); export orders from "friendly allies"; Q3 revenue ≥ ₹250 cr.
- **Key risks / de-rating:** Tender timing (single buyer, MoD); the FY27–28 cumulative **₹4,000 cr revenue target** (cited by an analyst on the Q1 call and not disputed) looks out of reach with H1 at ₹487 cr; working capital (257 days at Q1); other income falling as QIP cash is deployed (₹16.7 cr vs ₹25.3 cr YoY). **Severity: Medium.**
- **Abnormal / standout:** +144% QoQ revenue; FTP orders delivered ahead of schedule.
- **Catalysts:** Q2 transcript (H2 revenue and inflow outlook); DAC-to-RFP conversion; Q3 execution.
- **Expectations read:** **Ahead.** ~73× TTM (and well above the stock report's ₹1,150–1,200 zone) prices ~45% revenue CAGR. One strong quarter is consistent with that but does not prove it.
