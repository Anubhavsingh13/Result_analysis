## Verdict
**Concall Signal: 🔵 Thesis Intact (leaning positive)**
**Management Credibility: A** (specific, quantified, conservative; explains its misses before they happen)
**Results Verdict:** Print **In-line to Strong** · Quality **High** · Trajectory **Stable-to-improving**

A clean quarter on the things HCLTech controls. EBIT margin rose to **18.2%** (19.0% ex-restructuring, +131bp QoQ), bookings were **$3.3 bn** (second-highest ever, +27% YoY), Advanced AI revenue hit **$219 m (+78% YoY)**, and FCF conversion is still ~87% of net income. What it is *not* is a growth re-acceleration. Company revenue grew **+2.6% YoY in constant currency** (services +4%), software fell 11% YoY on renewal timing, and FY27 guidance was only tweaked: 2.5–4% now *includes* ~87bp of acquisitions, so the organic midpoint is unchanged. The INR print (+11.5% YoY revenue, +21% PAT) is mostly rupee depreciation (₹95.6 vs ₹87.4/$). At ~20× TTM P/E with an ~89% payout, the stock already prices a steady compounder. This quarter confirms that view without changing it.

## The 3 Things That Matter
1. **AI is starting to show up in margins, not just slides.** The CFO's bridge credits +73bp to Project Ascend/AI efficiencies and +32bp to higher-margin Advanced AI. CEO: Advanced AI services margins run *"at least 7% to 10% higher than traditional services"*; IP/platform gross margins are *"upwards of 70%"*.
2. **Growth is still low single digit, and partly bought.** HPE Telco ($21 m, two months) and Jaspersoft ($15 m) added 97bp to QoQ growth. Organic services growth was ~2% QoQ. Revenue per employee +2.4% YoY while headcount rose 6,450, so AI deflation is being offset, not escaped.
3. **Guidance discipline over optimism.** Services guidance was held at 3–4% (*"we feel confident to be slightly above the midpoint"*) despite 4% YoY services growth and record H1 bookings. CEO: *"we are in a volatile world, and we don't want to increase our guidance."* Q3 has a seasonal software step-up, so H2 risk is skewed to the upside.

## Key Investor Insights
- **Growth drivers:** Advanced AI at an $876 m run-rate (~6% of revenue, growing ~28% QoQ). AI-factory builds (+64% QoQ). Agentic AI >$100 m/quarter. Premier partnerships with Anthropic, OpenAI and NVIDIA. Retail/CPG +14%, financial services +7.1% YoY.
- **Re-rating triggers:** Advanced AI passing ~10% of revenue; software returning to growth in Q3 (ARR +7.8% says the business is healthy); FY28 margin framework pointing to 19%+.
- **Key risks / de-rating:** Telco/manufacturing client ramp-downs (three of the top-20 flagged). Europe −0.3% YoY. AI deflation outpacing new AI spend. US PERM suspension (management: *"no material impact"*). **Severity: Low–Medium.**
- **Abnormal / standout:** Bookings +35% QoQ to $3.3 bn, strongest H1 ever. The two largest Advanced AI deals were *"single sourced"*.
- **Catalysts (next 1–2 qtrs):** Q3 software seasonality; FY28 margin commentary (Jan); HPE Telco integration (−25bp margin drag this quarter).
- **Expectations read:** In line. ~20× P/E with an 89% payout prices 6–8% EPS growth plus yield. Upside needs AI-led growth above 5% CC.

## 2. Guidance & Outlook
- **Hard:** FY27 company CC growth **2.5–4%** (incl. ~87bp inorganic); services **3–4%** (incl. ~62bp inorganic); EBIT **17.5–18.5%** (unchanged; includes 40–50bp restructuring).
- **Soft:** *"We saw healthy demand towards the end of the quarter… so we remain cautious."* Q3 *"will be a strong quarter due to the Software step-up."*
- **vs prior call:** Q1 guidance was organic **1–4%**. Now 2.5–4% including acquisitions, so the organic range narrowed and the midpoint is *"broadly unchanged"* (CFO). Margin reaffirmed.

## 3. Management Language Read
- **Candid:** volunteered the three top-20 client headwinds (*"Two telco customers where discretionary spending was under pressure, one Manufacturing customer where there is a steep ramp-down"*) and pre-explained the software dip with a disclosure (multi-year license revenue $81 m → $39 m).
- **Measured:** declined to raise guidance despite the beat. Low promotional content outside the long AI section.
- **Watch:** the AI narrative dominated about half of the prepared remarks. Advanced AI is still only ~6% of revenue.
→ **A.**
